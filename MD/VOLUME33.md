## Proposal for printing Misrepresentations Proposal for Misrepresentations

Jonathan EdwardsProposal for printing *Misrepresentations Corrected*. N.d., [1752]. ANTS, f. ND5.7.

Proposal for printing by Subscription, a Piece

drawn up by the Rev. Mr. Jonath. Edwards of

Stockbridge, entitled, Misrepresentations Cor-

-rected and Truth Vindicated. being a Reply

to Mr. Williams of Lebanon's Book, entitled

The true state of the Question. wherein Mr.

The Controversy concerning the Terms of Christian

Communion is more exactly & clearly handled,

& in a more easy method ; The various Parts &

Principles of Mr. Williams's Scheme, are thorough-

ly examined and the Reasonings to support

Them are thoroughly examined.

This Piece is near finish'd, & will be put to the

Press assoon as a sufficient Number of Subscriptions

is obtaind. It will be of about the same Bulk

with Mr. Edwards's former discourse on this subject,

and Care will be taken that the Printer shall be

agreed with on as easy & reasonable Terms as may

be. --- They that are willing to encourage the

printing this Book are despired hereto to subscribe

their Names, with the number of Books they are

willing to take.
