Notes on the Comparison between the Sermon Text and the Printed Text of 1738:

Jeffrey Hensley (December, 1991)

1. Primarily, the changes from the sermon text to the published text are improvements of style (i.e. changing passive voice to active voice, eliminating wordiness, etc.) or ellaborations of a point for clarity. Also all scripture quotations are given in full, and a few new scripture passages are added.

2. The major differences between the texts are in the following sections: (page numbers match the printed text of 1738, in the Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, part of the original Yale Library of 1742)

A. p. 181 section V is a major addition to, reworking and relocation of the sermon version on L. 6v.

B. pp. 186-7 is an ellaboration of the state of the sheeps and the goats not given in the sermon text.

C. p. 187 to the end of the page is an expansion of Point #2 in the sermon.

D. Note changes in the numeration of points:

ex: #4 in sermon is #3 in the published text

E. Note the explanation of the background and context of Numbers 10:29 in the published text that is not in the sermon text.

F. p. 189 contains the addition of Revelation 19:7-9 not found in the sermon text.

G. Matt. 28:6 is in the sermon notes (L. 13v.) but is not in the published text.
