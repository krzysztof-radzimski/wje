marginal Directions how to find the Cranberry Place at Hog Island

After I Get over Wiltons meadow brook and Come to the ascent of the Hill about 20 or thirty Rod beyond the Brook to take the Left hand Path to Go up the Hill and about 10 or 15 Rod beyond the ascent of the Hill there I have mark'd a Pine Tree at the Left hand near a Rod from the Path by Beating off the Bark with a stone to the wood a Round spot Like a mark to shoot at and Laid several stones together at the Root of the Tree. in a Line from that Tree to the Bottom of the Descent from the Peak of mount Tom, is a Tall spruce Tree It may be about 40 Rod out of the Path the spot of Cranberries is about 7 Rod Northeast from that spruce Tree Just under a small White oak Tree the Top of which seems to be dead & broken off which stands on the West side of it & just by it may be two Rod off North west Lies a dry Log the stump being toward the northeast, that End that Lies toward the s. w. Lying up from the Ground

A.D. 1736. May the 5 was the first afternoon I did without fire

May 11 was the first day that any blossoms appeared on our cherry Trees & there were observable no blossoms on the Peach Trees tho they in other years used to blossom first.

May 12. had fire only Early in the morning & it was a very warm Day so that I kept a window open Great Part of the Day . & from this Time Left off Keeping fires

May 14 the blossoms began to appear on some of our apple Trees & Plumb trees. & It Grew warmer & warmer from this Time & in a very little Time after this became steadily very Hot weather

May A. D. 1737. May 7. was the first Day that blossoms began to appear on the Cherry trees before my House.
