Appendix: Dated Batches of Sermons 1723–1729

Dating by Thomas A. Schafer

Sermons are listed according to approximate date of composition and the original Schafer number, followed by the title if printed, or by the Scripture text, a statement of the doctrine, and any other relevant information if unpublished. Within period groups, sermons are listed in canonical order. In some cases, more precise dating of a sermon consequent to its original numbering has been noted in its description. Sermon texts followed by an asterisk indicate those printed in previous editions. Unless stated otherwise, the manuscript is in the Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University.



Fall 1723 25. Psalms 115:1. "It is the spirit and temper of those that are truly godly, to delight to exalt God and to lay themselves low before him." 26. A Spiritual Understanding of Divine Things Denied to the Unregenerate (1 Corinthians 2:14). MS in the Trask Library, Andover Newton Theological School, Newton Center, Mass.
Fall 1723–Winter 1724 27. The Pleasantness of Religion (Proverbs 24:13–14[a]). 28. Romans 1:24. "God sometimes punishes sin by giving men up to sin." 29. Living Peaceably One With Another (Romans 12:18). 30. Nothing Upon Earth Can Represent the Glories of Heaven (Revelation 21:18[a]). See also no. 34. 31. Ezekiel 7:16. "At a time when a people are called to a general humiliation, it becomes each one to mourn for his own iniquity." Fast-day sermon for April 15, 1724.
Summer 1724 32. Psalms 119:60(a). "We ought to make religion our present and immediate business."

-- 544 --


Spring 1725–Spring 1726 33. Romans 12:1. "Christ will give rest to all such as are spiritually weary and burdened that come to him for it." Abandoned in the summer of 1726; sermon on Matthew 11:28 begun on the second page in 1727 but not completed until 1730. 34. 1 John 3:2(a). "The godly are designed for unknown and inconceivable happiness." Only three leaves survive, inserted in no. 30. 35. Revelation 14:18–19. "God sometimes defers the punishment of sinners till they are fully ripe for destruction."
Spring–Summer 1726 36. 1 Peter 1:15. "Christians are under special obligations to be universally holy in their lives." New application added in 1727. 37. John 15:5(a). "Christ Jesus is the original and fountain of all spiritual life and nourishment."
Fall 1726 38. Job 14:5. "God unalterably determines the limits of every man's life." 39. Psalms 147:1. "It's a very decent and comely thing that praise should be given to God." Thanksgiving sermon for Nov. 10, 1726.
Winter–Fall 1727 40. Luke 9:23(a). "If we would be Christ's disciples, it is necessary that we should deny ourselves." 41. Warnings of Future Punishment Don't Seem Real to the Wicked (Genesis 19:14). 42. Deuteronomy 32:4. "God is a just and righteous God." Probably delivered in the spring of 1728. 43. Job 28:28. "Wisdom does above all things consist in godliness." 44. Psalms 139:7–10. "God is everywhere present." Probably delivered in the winter of 1728. 45. All God's Methods Are Most Reasonable (Isaiah 1:18–20). Three units, the first probably preached on Sept. 24, 1727. 46. Impending Judgments Averted Only by Reformation (Jonah 3:10). Heading: "Upon the fast for the earthquake." The earthquake occurred on Oct. 29, 1727; this was the province-wide fast held on December 21. 47. Matthew 5:22. "The punishment and misery of wicked men in another world will be in proportion to the sin that they are guilty of." 48. Matthew 6:33(a). "Religion should be the main business of our lives."

-- 545 --

49. Matthew 21:5. "The external meanness of Christ's appearance in the world is a thing worthy of the highest admiration." Incorporates the application from no. 24. 50. Matthew 24:35. "God never fails of his word." 51. Luke 2:14(a). "The Son of God, by appearing in our nature, laid a glorious foundation for peace to the inhabitants of this world." 52. Luke 2:14(b). "Christ's appearing in our nature is an instance of wonderful love to man." This and no. 51 constitute a two-sermon series in two booklets. 53. Luke 13:24. "There are many that seek eternal life, that will never obtain." Only the last four pages are extant; the doctrine is taken from an auditor's notes (Trask Library), which place the sermon in Sept. 1727. 54. Luke 17:9. "God don't thank men for doing those things which he commands them." 55. True Nobleness of Mind (Acts 17:11). 56. 2 Corinthians 4:7. "God is pleased to make his own power appear, by carrying on the work of his grace by such instruments as men, that in themselves are utterly insufficient for it." Begun early in 1727 and completed and preached in 1729, shortly after Stoddard's death, possibly as an installation sermon. 57. 2 Corinthians 6:10. "A Christian possesses all things." 58. 2 Corinthians 7:10. "No man will ever have occasion to repent his forsaking sin and turning to God."


Winter 1728 59. Galatians 5:6(a). "Only that sort of faith that works by love avails anything before God." 60. Deuteronomy 32:29. "Such persons are very imprudent and foolish, that don't consider their latter end." 61. Psalms 102:25–26. "This present world shall one day come to an end." Probably for a special occasion in January or February 1728; alludes to earthquake of October 1727. 62. Hosea 13:9(a). Not extant, but mentioned in no. 63. Probable form of the doctrine: "Those sinners that are destroyed, it is they destroy themselves." 63. Hosea 13:9(b). "Those sinners that are saved, it is God saves them." 64. Habakkuk 1:13(a). "God hates sin." Heading: "Orlando Bridgman"; preached on an occasion of ecclesiastical discipline, probably for drunkenness. 65. 2 Corinthians 8:9. "Our Lord Jesus Christ was rich, but for our sakes he

-- 546 --

became poor, that we through his poverty might be rich." Probably delivered in the spring of 1728.

 66. James 1:17. "God is the Father of lights."
Spring 1728 67. Psalms 90:12. "Our time here is so short and uncertain, that we had great need wisely to improve it." 68. Daniel 4:35. "God doth whatever he pleases." 69. Matthew 10:28. "The bodies of wicked men, as well as their souls, will be punished forever in hell." 70. Luke 5:5–6. "It is our best way in all cases, to follow the directions that Christ gives us." 71. Romans 2:16. "The secrets of men must all come into judgment before God." 72. 2 Corinthians 3:18(a). "1. Believers have such a sight of the glory of Christ as revealed in the gospel, as changes 'em into a likeness to the same glory. 2. 'Tis the work of the Spirit of Christ only, to give a true sight of Christ and to make like to Christ." Two sermons, one on each doctrine. 73. Frag. C. The last ten pages of what may have been the doctrinal part of the election-day sermon for May 29, 1728.
Summer 1728–Winter 1729 74. 2 Kings 7:3–4. "A possibility of being saved is much to be preferred to a certainty of perishing." 75. Psalms 65:11. "We ought to praise God for annual blessings." Thanksgiving sermon for November 7, 1728. 76. Proverbs 4:23. "If we have any regard to our salvation, it concerns us with all diligence to keep our hearts." 77. Proverbs 17:27. "The spirit of true godliness is an excellent spirit." 78. Ecclesiastes 9:10(a). "Persons had need to make all possible haste, to get that work done that must be done and that can't be done after death." 79. Canticles 1:3(a). "Christ Jesus is a person transcendently excellent and desirable." 80. Isaiah 32:2.∗ "There is in Christ Jesus abundant foundation of peace and safety for those that are in fear and danger….2. There is in Christ provision for the satisfaction and full contentment of the needy and thirsty soul….3. There is quiet rest and sweet refreshment in Christ Jesus for him that is weary." One sermon on each proposition. Dwight ed., 8, 355–78.

-- 547 --

81. Living Unconverted Under an Eminent Means of Grace (Jeremiah 6:29–30). Preached shortly after Stoddard's death. 82. Matthew 12:30. "There are no neuters in religion." 83. Matthew 13:22(a). "The cares of this life are what often hinders any saving effect of the Word of God." 84. Profitable Hearers of the Word (Matthew 13:23). Three sermons. 85. Luke 8:28. "There is no goodness in praying, though it be never so earnestly, merely out of fear of misery." 86. The Spiritual Blessings of the Gospel Represented by a Feast (Luke 14:16). Sacrament sermon. MS in Trask Library. 87. The Torments of Hell Are Exceeding Great (Luke 16:24). 88. Luke 17:34. "There will be a great difference made hereafter in the state of many that have lived together here." MS in Trask Library. 89. Luke 19:42(a). "If sinners outstand their day of grace, they will surely perish." 90. 1 Corinthians 16:22. "It's crime enough to render any man a cursed person, not to love Jesus Christ." 91. Galatians 2:20. "Christ had a particular respect to every believer, in what he did and suffered in the work of redemption." 92. 1 John 4:19. "God is first in love to his saints." 93. Isaiah 53:3(a). "Our Lord Jesus Christ, when upon earth, met with abundance of contempt and reproach." 94. Jeremiah 42:20. "There are some men do nothing but dissemble, in their pretenses of disposedness to their duty." Sacrament sermon. 95. Amos 8:11. "Spiritual judgments are the most terrible that can befall a people." Fast-day sermon for March 20, 1729. 96. None Are Saved by Their Own Righteousness (Titus 3:5).


Spring–Fall 1729 97. Exodus 20:24. "If we would be in the way of God's grace and blessing, we must wait upon him in his own way and in the use of his appointed means." 98. Psalms 66:5. "'Tis to the glory of God, that he is terrible in his doings towards the children of men." 99. Psalms 82:6–7. "Great men are as liable to death as others." 100. Proverbs 5:11–13(a). "Vicious persons, when once they come to taste the bitter fruits of their evil courses, will greatly lament their folly in not hearkening to the counsels and warnings that were given them."

-- 548 --

101. The Threefold Work of the Holy Ghost (John 16:8). 102. Romans 6:14. "The gospel, or new covenant, is eminently a dispensation of grace." 103. 1 Corinthians 1:9. "Christians have communion with Christ." Sacrament sermon. 104. 1 Peter 1:3. A sermon on the resurrection of Christ. Not extant but cited in "Miscellanies" no. 409. 105. Revelation 3:15. "A man had better be a heathen, than to differ from 'em only in common light and profession."


Summer 1729. 106. Deuteronomy 7:10. "God will not be slack in punishing wicked men."
Summer–Fall 1729 107. Numbers 23:19. "God never changes his mind." 108. Deuteronomy 32:35(a). "The punishment of wicked men generally comes upon them when they don't expect it." 109. 2 Samuel 23:5. "The covenant of grace is every way so ordered as is needful, in order to its being made firm and sure." 110. Job 31:3. "It is a strange punishment that God has assigned to the workers of iniquity." 111. Psalms 18:26. "They that contend with God, can expect no other than that God will contend with them." 112. Psalms 33:1. "The righteous, above other men, have reason to praise God." 113. The Sacrifice of Christ Acceptable (Psalms 40:6–8). 114. God's All-Sufficiency for the Supply of Our Wants (Psalms 65:9). Thanks giving sermon for Nov. 13, 1729. 115. Sin and Wickedness Bring Calamity and Misery on a People (Proverbs 14:34). Preached before the county court, probably in early December 1729. 116. Proverbs 23:5. "Earthly riches are of a very fleeting nature." 117. Canticles 5:1. "Persons need not and ought not to set any bounds to their spiritual and gracious appetites." Sacrament sermon. 118. Canticles 8:1. "The incarnation of Jesus Christ was a thing greatly longed for by the church." 119. Isaiah 3:1–2. "It's a manifestation of God's displeasure against a people, when he takes away from them many that have been useful and serviceable among them." Mentions Stoddard's ministry and death; possibly for a fast day. 120. Glorying in the Savior (Isaiah 45:25).

-- 549 --

121. Jeremiah 5:9. "'Tis unreasonable to think that God will take no care to punish the wickedness that there is in the world." 122. Jeremiah 17:5–6(a). "They that trust in the creature will be left in an utterly destitute and hopeless condition, and shall not see when good comes." 123. Jeremiah 17:7–8. "They that trust in God have a full fountain of good to live upon, that won't fail in evil and calamitous times." Sequel to no. 122. 124. Matthew 7:14(a). "The entrance into eternal life is a strait and narrow passage." 125. Matthew 15:26. "There is a great difference between converted and unconverted men." 126. Matthew 24:43. "Many wicked men, if they knew how near death was, would not do as they do." Probably a funeral sermon. 127. Luke 13:30. "Some that seem to be first among professors in this world will appear to be last in the other world; and so on the contrary, some that are last will be first." 128. Luke 22:48. "There are those that make an outward show of friendship to Jesus Christ, that at the same time act the part of enemies and betrayers." 129. John 8:44(a). "Wicked men are the children of the devil." 130. Ephesians 5:1. "1. It is very becoming of and essential to a child of God, that he should be conformed to his heavenly Father. 2. Childlike respect to God should be the main principle of the Christian's obedience." Edwards makes two observations on the text but uses only the second as his doctrine; it is not clear whether there was a sermon on the first. 131. 1 Peter 1:8(a). "They live the happiest life in this world, that live a life of love to Jesus Christ, beholding him with an eye of faith." 132. 1 Peter 2:2–3. "If persons have ever tasted the sweetness of the word and grace of Christ, they will be longing for more and more of it." 133. 1 Peter 3:4. "A Christian spirit is of great price in the sight of God." 134. 2 Peter 1:16. "Seeing the glory of Christ is what tends to assure the heart of the truth of the gospel." 135. 2 Peter 2:14. "Men's being so addicted to any wicked practice that they can't leave it off, it is no excuse to 'em but an aggravation." 136. Job 1:5."1. 'Tis the part of wise and pious parents, to maintain a true concern of mind towards their children, lest they should fall into sin. 2. The commission of wickedness against light and

-- 550 --

knowledge has this tendency, to cause men to curse God in their hearts." Two short sermons, one on each observation.

 137. Job 34:21. "God sees and particularly observes all men's behavior in the world." 138. The Day of Judgment (Acts 17:31).∗ Worcester rev. ed., 4, 202–25.
