Claimed the author8ty & Jurisdiction of one universal P that was appointed by God appointed by God to watch over the whole Church & declared that his Pastoral vigilance was extend- restrained to no Bounds but that it extended to all Places where Christ is Known and adored.

About the year . 434. in the Time of sixtus. III. Bp. of Rome. the r reading of the SS. by the commonPeople began to be discountenanced vol . 2 . p. 4.

Leo the great Pretended that the Jewish Precepts with Respect to Fasts & several other Practices are binding under the Gospel dispensation & excluded those from Orders who had married widows . about the year . 440.

This Pope extended the Law of Celibacy to the subdeacons who nevertheless were not to abandon the wives they had married but changing the carnal into a spiritual marriage live with them not as wives but as sister. This Law however did not universally obtain even in the suburbicarian provinces 'till long after Leo's Time nay some of his successours and among the Rest Gregory the Great thought it very hard that subdeacons should be debarred all Commerce with [col. 2] the wives they had married or even from marrying the Contrary Practice prevail'd in Gaul even in Leos Time as appears from the Canons of the first Council of Arles the second of Arles and that of Angers only forbidding the deacons to marry

In the year . 445 . Le Pope Leo. when Hilarius Bp. of Arles came to Rome to expostulate with Leo for pretending to set aside the decree of the Gallican Bishops in deposing Celidonius Bp. of Besanzon Leo caused Him to be seized and kept under an Arrest an Instance of violence which Rome had not yet seen with Respect to a Bishop and in matters not of Faith But of discipline . and after Hilarius had found means to escape Leo proceeded so far as to cut him off from the Communion of the Apostolic see deprived Him of all Jurisdiction over the seven Provinces over which He was Exert Exarch suspended Him from ordaining any Bishop or even assisting at the Ordination of any, and to be more fully revenged on Him utterly suppressed the dignity of Exarch annexed to the see of Arles . and afterwards inveigled that weak Emperour Valentinian IIId a Rescript vesting the Bps of Rome with an absolute and uncon trol trouled authority over the Gallican Chhs & Bishops . Commanding all the Bps to hold and observe as a Law whatever it shall please the Bp. of Rome to ordain & decree and strictly enjoin'd the magistrates to oblige those who should be summoned to Rome to obey the summons

In the year . 449. Pope Leo sending Renatus & Hilarius the former a Presbyter and the latter a deacon of the Roman Chh. to th as his[p. 4] Legates to the Council of Ephesus styled them his Legates de Latere suo and it was the first Time the Po Popes Legates were so styled . meaning that they were belonging to the Roman Chh. or under her immediate Jurisdiction.

In the year 449. The Emperour Valentinian with his Mother Placidia and his wife Endoxia came from Ravenna the usual Place of their Residence to visit the Chhs of the saints in Rome according to a superstitious Custom which then began to obtain about this Time

In the year 450 . Pope Leo having great Interest with the Emperour Marcian He promised Him i at his Request to assemble a general Council concerning the Controversy Concerning Eutiches and & his Friends on the one side & his opponents on the other & that the Pope should be absolute master of it

And in this Council which was the Council of Chald Chalcedon The Popes Legates afte held the first Place after the Emperours Commissioners above all the Rest of the Clergy which which till now was a new Thing & was now obtained be by this Pretence that the all the Bishops had before th judged the Cause and were of a Party

In this Council These Legates took upon them to Command in the in an authoritative style[col. 2] in the name of his Apostleship (as they styled the Bp. of Rome) what should be done.

And when dioscorus Bp. of Alexandria was deposed in by that Council the sentence was pronounced in Leo's name.

In the year 451. The G After the Council of Chalcedon had confirmed to the Bp. of Constantinople the P same Power & Jurisdiction over mo the Chhs of the East which the Bp. of Rome excercised over the Chhs of the west and declared him equal to the Bp. of Rome in every Thing but Precedency Leo the Bp. of Rome most violently & Resolutely opposed this decree & wrought so indefatigably with the Emperour & Martian & the Empress Pulcheria . that in submission to them the Patria Anatolius the Patriarch of Constantinople is brought meanly to own that the decree of the Council could not take P Effect without hi the B his Confirmation.

About the year 464. Pope Hilarius strove much to bring the Gallican Bp. into an absolute subjection to his Authority & in a great measure effected his Purpose

About 465. The same Pope has a favourable opportunity for bringing the spanish Bps into[p. 5] a greater subjection to Him & neglects not to improve it The Popes began now to act when the Emperours did not reside at Rome and the Power of the Emperours was at a very low Ebb. to act in all things as sovereigns of Rome

At this Time the worship of saints because began to prevail and Chapels & Churches were built & dedicated to their Honour.

In the year. 472. The John Bp. of Ravenna and metropolitan of the Prog Province of æmilia having ordaind Gregory P a Presbyter of Ravenna Bp. if Modena against his will Pope simplicius repr finding great fault with the Metropolitans Proceedings established Gregory Bp. of R Modena. But by an attempt on the known Rights of Metropolitans yet unprecedented he exempts him from all subjection to the Bp. of Ravenna and declares him immediately subject to the see of Rome

At the same time the same Pope took upon him alone to deprive the Bp. of Ansinum of the Power of ordination leaving h without the Advice or approbation of his suffragans assembled in Council which was a new usurpation contrary to what Rules & Custom which till then had remain'd establish'd

In the year 484. Pope Felix II excommunicated and anathematized Acacius Patriarch of Constantinople for his Ill treatmt of his Legates sent to Constantinople & other things and required[col. 2] all Xtians warned not to commu nicate with Him on Pain of an Anathema

on this Acacius also excommunicated the Pope and the Eastern Bps in General joined with Acacius whence followed a separa tion between the Chhs of the East & west

Nevertheless Fravitas the successor of Acacius wrote a flattering Letter to the Pope begging his Communion and owning st Peter to be the first of the Apostles to be the Foundation stone of the true Faith, and the Pope to hold the Faith which was held taught & preach'd by that Apostle this was in the year 489. But yet Pope Felix would not admit him to communion because the Name of Acacius was not struck out of the Dyptics

Euphemius Patriarch of Constantino ple. successour of Fravitas. sought the Communion of Felix but was Refused unless he would rase out of the dyptics the names of Acacius & Fravitas. A.D. 490.

Pope Gelasius by a decree first approved of the Apocryphal Books which decree was confirmed by a Council held at Rome A.D. 494 consisting of 70 . Bps. which Books neverthess were nt universally recited as Canonical among the Rom Catholicks til nor even By all the Popes 'till long after even till the Council of Trent

Anno . 1496 496 It was now high Time for the Popes to think of Changing the Foundation on which the Primacy of the Roman see had before stood The [p. 6] decrees of Councils Rome being only the metropolis of a small Kingdom the Kingd. of Italy and Constanstantinople the sole Imperial City in the whole Xtian world Therefore Pope Gelasius declared "That it was not any Councils or the decrees of any , That the holy Roman Catholick & apostolic Chh owed her Primacy But to the words of our saviour Thou art Peter &c and thereby building the Chh upon him as upon a Rock that Nothing could shake ; That the Roman Chh not having spot or wrinkle, was consecrated & exalted above all other Chhs by the Presence as well as by the death & martyrdom & glorious Triumph of the two Chief Apostles st Peter & st Paul who suffered at Rome under Nero, not at different Times as the Hereticks say , but at the same Time & at the same day . & that the Roman Chh is the first Chh because founded by the first Apostle the Chh of Alexandria the 2d because founded by his d. st Mark in his name & that of Antioch the third because st Peter dwelt there before he came to Rome & in that City the faithful were first distinguished with the name of Xtians

It was in the year 501. In the Time of Pope symmachus That the Notion of the Independency of the Pope upon Councils was broached on this occasion. The Pope being accused of many Crimes by the Party that had opposed him at his Election & had been the Friends of his Compe[ti]tour Laurentius The King of[col. 2] the Goths order a Council of the Bps of Italy to meet to Judge him But the Pope on one pretence or other declined appearing before them &c - on which his Partizans advanced this notion That no Assembly of Bps had Power to judge the Pope That He was accountable for his actions to God alone [* next Col.

About Pope suy symmachus's Time who began his Popedom Anno 498 . first was dropped the Custom of taking the voice of the People as well as Clergy in the Election of a Pope

In the year 502. In the Time of Pope syp symmachus a council of Italian Bps Convened at Rome it was determined that no Civil magistrate or Layman had Power to concern Themselves in the Goods & Estate of the Chh. and the Clergy only were called the Chh tho formerly the whole Con gregation of the Faithful had been so called.

In the year 502. Pope symmachus has the assurance as to tell the Ep Emperour Anastasius that the successour of st Peter is at least as great as an Emperour

* last Col.] one Ennodius afterwards[p. 7] Bp. of Paviawrote in defence of this in which in He will not allow that there ever can be Room for Reproof. Censure or Controul with Respect to the Pope for the Papal dignity says He either finds or makes saints all who are raised to it which Apology of Ennodius was afterwards in the year 503. Con firmed by a Council held at Rome. extolled it with the highest Commendations and ordered it to be inserted among the Acts of the Council and to be deemed of as much authority as the Council it self or any decreed made by the Council And the Pope not satisfied with this commanded with the unanimous Consent of the Bps that it should be placed among the decrees of the Apostolic see to be held by all as one of them

In the Great C year 517. many of the mon In the Great Quarrel between the Emperour Anastasius & Pope Hormisdas Many of the monks of the East being oppressed by the Emperour & the Bps of the East for their adherence to the Pope They recur to the Pope for Relief desiring him to exercise the Power of the Keys on their Behalf styling him the most holy & blessed Patriarch of the whole Θ. Anno 15 519 . The Emperour Justin 519 being a great Friend to the Pope at Length complied with him not only complied with Hormisdas's unreasonable demands of Erasing out of the dyptics the names of Acacius a former Pa triarch of Constantinople for[col. 2] affronting the then Popes Legates & in other Respects so as exceedingly to thwart the App ambitous views of the Papacy & not only so but also to gratify the Popes Revenge so so & Pride so far as to rase out the names not only of Acacius but but all the other Patriarchs who had succeeded him ever since the Beginning of the schism o viz Fravitas Euphemius Macedonius & Timothius. & also to promist to conform in all things to the Apostolick see & to Look on all dying out of her communion as dying out of the communion of the Catholick Chh. & not only complied Himself but forced the Bp. of Constantinople to comply with the same & so re- restored union between the Chhs of Rome & Constantinople after a separation of 35. year . He endeavoured to force all the Bps in his dominions also to comply to these Terms The Patriarch of Antioch complied but most the Bps of his the East Refused to reject tho they complied with Acacius refused with Regard to his successours . & the Emperour at last gained with them & the Pope seeing it in vain to struggle pre tended to acquiesce & so union was Restored between the Chh of Rome & the Chhs of the East on the Terms of rasing the name of Acacius only Hormisdas says Mr. Bowers was[p. 8] the first Xtian Bp. who in matters of Conscience, dared openly to Countenenance nay & to sanctify slaughter & Bloodshed.

Hormisdas being applied to about some Point of discipline by John of Tarragon & salustius of seville He thence took occasion to appoint them his vicars the former for the hither & the latter for the farther spain impowering them to assemble in Council the Bp. of the Provinces over which they were set to Compose their differences to see that the Canons were every where punctually observed . This Power he granted them on Condition of their recurring to him in all matter of momt Thus by their means He extended his Power over all the Provinces & Bps of spain . The Institution of vicars was of all the devices that the most refined Policy could invent the last calculated for the establishing the Papal authority . The Bps of the first sees who were the most capable effectually of opposing the Encroachmts of Rome being hereby ingaged in Gratitude as well as Interest to support & defend it

In the Time of Pope Felix III . King Athalaric Go made a law in favour of the Ecclesiasticks obliging Lay men in all suits at Law to bring their Action first before the Bishop yet allowing an Appeal from the Judgmt of the Bp. to[col. 2] that of the secular Court at the same Time Alarick King of the visigoths in Gaul made a Law still more favourable to the Clergy for fo by his Law no Lay-man was to sue an Ecclesiastic in a secular Court nor was an Ecclesiastic to answer any action brought against him there without the Permission of the Bp. . The Priviledge granted by Athalaric to the Roman Clergy only Justinian extended Emperour of the East extended to all Ecclesiastics

That Kind of Idolatry which consists in swearing by Creatures pieces of the Cross Relick st Peters Keys & saints &c seems to have Crept into the Chh. in the Time of the Empe rour Justinian who began to Reign Anno 527 . & died 565.

About the year 592. Pope Gregory the Great began to raise the Papal authority by vigorously & resolutely asserting and exercising all the authority & Prerogatives that any of one of his Predecessours had ever Claimed after the Papal authority had sunk m[p. 9] low by the weakness & imprudent conduct of many of his Predecessours But otherwise He was a man of Great Justice meekness modesty & Clemency & the [most] vertuous Pope that had appeared for several Centuries Past

Gregory the Great when the Britains were converted by Austin Commanded that the new Converts should be indulged in the use of their old Heathenish Rites sacrificing &c. but only be directed to the True God so the Converts from many other nations were indulged in Like manner by which means among others multitudes of Heathen Ceremonies were introduced into the Xtian Chh . The Franks settled in the south of Gaul used to retain their Images in the Houses of worship serenus their Bp. commanded 'em to be destroyed ut Gregory Reproved him much for it In Anno 601. Image worship in the Xtian Chh was now in its Infancy

Bowers observes that in the year 601. The Britons the Picts & the Scots had ever maintained an absolute independence on the Bp. of Rome & that they would not join with the Converts of the saxons or English made by Austin & others because of their Great Corruptions still Themselves still retaining the antient Plainness & simplicity[col. 2] of worship.

Gregory the Great says Bowers on the account of his vertues & the vices of his successours is styled by many the Last Bp of Rome

Gregory the Great was the first that discovered Purgatory and it was by means of the Apparitions & visions that He relates from others pa which seem to be some ignorant People which he Conversed with

Bowers's History seems much to confirm that with the Pontificate of Boniface the III which began Feb 19. 607. Began the Reign of Antix that Great Period this was the true Epoch of the 1260 days . For He first began to Advance the Power & usurpati of the Papal see after it had long been at a stand yea after its authority had greatly declined for many successions till Gregory the Great who supported but did not advan the all Power that h any of his Predecessours claimed Before Him were some Popes in many Respects Good & vertrous but none after Just before this Time The Image worship worship & the Idolatrous Rites of the Heathen Barbarous Nations of the Heathens began to be introduced & established in the Chh. Purgatory just before was discovered He was made Pope Chiefly by the Instumentality of Phocas the [p. 10] one of the usurper one of the most unjust [-] cruel & every way wicked Princes that ever was Tho' he Possessed th

Bowers observes that tho' he Possessed the see not a year yet to him to him alone the Roman see owes more than to all his Predecessours together. He prevailed on Phocas to revoke the decree settling the Title of Universal Bp. on the Bp. of the Imperial City But obtained a new decree settling that Title on Himself & his successours Pope Gregory who who was the next Predecessour but one to Boniface Had Hims. after declared that whoever should presume in the Pride of his Heart to take that Title to Himself was a Follower of satan a Rival of Satan in Pride & the Forerunner of Antix, & called it a Badge of Pride and mark of Antix . Phocas not only gave Bonifact this Title But He was withal as all Historians agree declared the Head of the whole Catholic . Chh. And Boniface had scarce obtained this Title when he took it upon to exercise an Answerable Jurisdiction & Power, a Jurisdiction & Power to that Time unknown & unheard of in the Catholic Chh. He immediatly Assembled a Council in the Basilic of st Peter conisting of 72 Bps 34. Presbyters & all the deacons & inferiour Clergy of that City And acted there with all the Authority of abso supreme[col. 2] Head or rather absolute monarch of the Chh For by a decree which He issued in that Council it was pronounced declared & defined that no Election of a Bp. should thenceforth be deemed lawful & good unless made by the People & Clergy approved by the Prince or Lord of the City by the Pope interposing his Authority in the following Terms We will & Command "And then says Bowers was the Power of the Pope as Universal Bp as Head of the Chh or in other words the Papal supremacy first Introduced It owed its Original to the worst of men, was procured by the Basest means by flattery a Tyrant in his wickedness & Tyranny and was in it self if we stand to the Judgmt of Gregory the Great Antixtian, Heretical, Blasphemous diabolical"

This Beast Rose of out of the Tempestuous sea agreeable to SS. out of the great Tumult, of of the Emperour Empire & the exceeding instability Faction & Rebellion of the multitude

About 7 years after this Pope Anno 614. That Mahomet first Published his Law . having set up for a Prophet 4 years before concerning the Rise of Antix. see further p. 19. * e. &c. & p. 21. . d.

The Pall which metropolitans[p. 11] were wont to receive of the Popes was made use of as a means of a gradual great Increase of the Popes Grandeur Power & Riches . - The Pall was originally part of the Imperial Habit Tis said the Bps of Rome were allowed to wear it by Constantine the great and in process of Time the same Honour was granted by the Emperours to the other Patriarchs Before the Pontificate of Gregory the Great Chosen in 590.
