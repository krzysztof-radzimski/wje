Fragment I.I OS 157-179 Jonathan Edwards

Fragment I of Original Sin

Possibly part of a penultimate or at least a late draft of Edwards' defense of the doctrine of original sin, this manuscript fragment corresponds to the last third or so of the printed treatise, beginning on p. 250, part-way through Pt. II, Ch. IV, sect. I, and continuing to the end. While the basic form of the finished product is here, there are significant differences between this draft and the printed text, which nicely illustrate Edwards' process of composition.11

***

The manuscript is an infolded folio quire of eighteen sheets (Maid of Dordt/GRwr) stitched at the fold. Edwards numbered the pages 157-224. There are also several smaller pieces—salvaged letter covers and other scraps—either stitched or pinned in, on which Edwards wrote insertions cued to neighboring pages. The last leaf, which is missing the top third, is unnumbered and blank, and probably served as a cover.
