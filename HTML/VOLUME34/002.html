Fragement II OS 045-54 Jonathan Edwards

Fragment II of the Treatise on Original Sin

Parts of two drafts of Edwards' final treatise on original sin have survived. The piece presented here contains most of Pt. I, Ch. I, Sect. VIII, on "The Native Depravity of Mankind Appears, in That There Has Been so Little Good Effect of so Manifold and Great Means, Used to Promote Virtue in the World," parallelling pages 77-95 in the first edition.11 Here, Edward reviews the "obstinacy" of the heathen and of the Jews in their continuing unbelief and apostacy, despite the testimony of holy prophets and kings, as well as divine appearances, deliverances, and judgments, exacerbated by their resistance to the gospel.

*****

These five folio sheets (Maid of Dordt/GRcr) are so neatly written and—almost unheard of in Edwards' MSS—conscientiously punctuated as to be fugitives from a penultimate draft, or, even more rare, from the fair copy for the printer. On page 46, in the left margin, appears a notation in a different hand, "p. 81G," that refers to the corresponding place in the printed text, which suggests this was in the hands of a printer.22 Differences between this MS and the first edition of 1758 are provided in the notes. Minor differences, such as spelled-out ampersands and punctuation, are not noted.
