if Persons Let them means alone yet they wont Let them alone

and dont Concern themselves to Improve them yet what

Improvement they shall make of them yet means wont

Let them alone they will have some effect upon them

but it will Evermore be to their hurt .

Thus if persons Come to meeting and attend Preaching &

ordinances in a Careless & negligent . not Regarding

how they attend or What they hear it spending their

time in Gazing about the meeting . H. or sit

sleeping Great Part of the time they Evermore Prove

for their hurt not if they attend after such a manner
