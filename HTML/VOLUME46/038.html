Pull down Gods Kingdom in the T . they harbour their

vile Lusts those viperous Enemies of G. in their hearts

all the while they hant forsaken their sin nor wont

forsake it nor have never Yet Repented truly of

what they have done against G. & never parted with

nor will they do it.

& yet they think that G. should have Respect to them

for their Religion & should Pity & morality & that he

should Pity em for their Prayers & that he should Give

them spiri Converting Grace the Greatest Gift that

Ever G. bestowed on any Creature for their seeking

their own deliverance from damnation.
