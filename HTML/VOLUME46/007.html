of Gods sp. for all spiritual prospoerity Labour to have forth In

creased as a sense of your own nothingness or & more & more

Increased and a sense of fullness & freeness of the Grace of

G. & often seek ye G. By Prayer humble & fervent Prayer

for his Graciou the dews of his sp. & in that way Keep alwaies

keep your self waiting on G. for them .
