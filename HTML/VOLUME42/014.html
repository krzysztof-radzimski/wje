we Are not thankfull for the mercies of the Year

Past. And tho God is indeed kind to the Unthank

full and Evil yet so much the stronger obligati

ons are Upon us to thankfullness to that being thats

is so Good and mercifull. for these Reasons

Let us be Perswaded to Praise God

___________

1 in Our Closets. how Comely is it for the humble

Xtian. with his heart full of holy Joy and Cheer-

fullness. to Enter into his Closet. and on his knees

offer Up his heart, a sacrifice, Burning in the

flame of love. for God loves to behold such a sight

and to Recieve such sacrifices. Let our Closets and Pla-

ces of Retirement be witnesses of our sincere and hear-

ty Praises. Let us Offer such offerings in secret and

God will Reward us openly

______________________________

2 Let families be Exhorted truly to Praise God. how

Comely is it. for those whose Interest is so United &

have together experienced the same bounty of God marginal

Unitedly to offer their Praise to him. who is so Good

a Father to us. from Whom Come all family as

well as personal blessings.

Let Every Family therefore this day Call to mind

the blessings of heaven that have descended upon

them. Let Each as they Let each family as they

may this day Partake of the Redundant Goodness

of God Remember the fountain of all. Joining

their hearts in the same Joy and love to their bene

factor Let. Antiently Every family had a priest

to offer their than sacrifices and thankofferings therefo

as Abel, Abraham Isaac and Jacob. but Xtians

are all of them Priests. 1 Peter 2,5 Ye also as

lively stones are built up a spiritual house an

holy Priesthood to offer up spiritual sacrifices ac

ceptable to God by Jesus X. & 9 v. but ye are a

Chosen Generation a Royal Priesthood, Let therefore

Every one this day exercise himself in his function

and offer up his sacrifices but Let him be sure that

they are Perfumed with the Incense that ascends

from the hand of X Rev 8.3,4

_____________________________________________________

3 Let us be sincere in Our Publick Praises How

Amiable for Gods People upon Recieving of Publick

mercies to Go up to his house with Joy upon their

heads. with hearts full of a sense of his Goodness

there to Join in Gods Praise. Let Every one see that

he bears a part in making spiritual Melody. Re-

members that God is Present & looks into Every heart

and distinguishes by the Eye of his Omniscience, be

tween such as Praise him with the heart. & such as

praise him with their heart and such as praise him on marginal

ly with their lips. how Much more Comely would

the Celebration of such times, and days of thanks

giving be if there were an air of sincerity in Every

one and if it Appeard by Our behaviour . then a Grat-

full Impression was made upon our minds. and we

were Really put upon Enquiring what we should

Render to the Lord for all his benefits towards us
