APPENDIX B
DATED SERMONS, JANUARY 1734–DECEMBER 1738

The following list records the extant dated sermons that Edwards preached from January 1734 through December 1738. Sermons are listed according to Edwards' date of composition and the original Schafer number, followed by the title if printed, or by the Scripture text, a statement of the doctrine, and any other relevant information if unpublished. Edwards usually dated his sermons by month and year; where the exact order of sermons within a given month cannot be ascertained, they are listed in canonical order. Sermon texts followed by an asterisk indicate those printed in previous editions. Unless stated otherwise, the manuscript is in the Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University.

311. Ecclesiastes 9:10 (c). "Whatever persons look upon needful to be done for their own salvation and intend to do at all they should do now." Sewed together with 307 (b) as a continuation of it. Jan. 1734. Originally stitched together with no. 307.

312. Isaiah 53:7 (a). "The meekness of Jesus Christ was especially manifested in his behavior under his last sufferings." Feb. 1734.

313. Matthew 6:2. "Hypocrites will never get anything by their religion but only what they get in this world." Feb. 1734.

314. Acts 16:29–31 (a). "Sinners under concern and distress of mind about the condition of their souls ought to take encouragement from the infinite mercy of God in Christ." Feb. 1734.

315. Heeding the Word, and Losing It (Hebrews 2:1). Feb. 1734.

316. Isaiah 5:4. "That it may well be asked concerning some persons that are unprofitable under means of grace, what God could have done more to them that he has not done." Mar. 1734.

317. John 14:2 (a). "Jesus Christ has dealt faithfully with us and has plainly told us the truth in all things that concern our welfare." Mar. 1734. Two or more sermons.

343. Justification by Faith Alone (Romans 4:5). Nov. 1734. Lecture.

353. Ruth's Resolution (Ruth 1:16). Apr. 1735.

354. Luke 15:28–31. "Prop. I, Persons that have been long religious are oftentimes much displeased that God shows mercy to others that have lived wicked lives and but lately begun in religion." Apr. 1735.

358. Ephesians 5:25–27. "Christ loved the church and gave himself for it that he might present it to himself in perfect purity, beauty, and glory." May 1735.

370. God Amongst His People (Isaiah 12:6). Nov. 1735. Thanksgiving sermon.

372. Jonah 2:4. "There is encouragement for persons that have been seeking mercy, though they are under never so dark and discouraging circumstances, yet to look again towards God's holy temple." Dec. 1735.

374. Psalms 65:2.* "'Tis the character of the Most High God, that he is a God that answers prayer." Jan. 1736. "Fast on occasion of the sickness at the East Ward." Published as The Most High a Prayer-Hearing God in Sermons (1788), pp. 67–85; repr. Worcester rev. ed., 4, 561–72.

375. Ezekiel 16:63. "God's people ought to be much in reflecting on their sins and humbling themselves for them after they have evidences of God's having forgiven them." Jan. 1736.

378. Psalms 116:12. "When persons have received signal mercies of God, it becomes 'em to be earnestly inquisitive what they shall render to the Lord for all his benefits towards them." Feb. 1736.

379. Ezekiel 33:4–5. "If men but be sensible of the need of it, they ordinarily have it in their power to take likely methods in order to their salvation." Feb. 1736. "Preached at Springfield."

388. Acts 19:19. "When the Spirit of God has been remarkably poured out on a people, a thorough reformation of those things that before were amiss amongst them ought to be the effect of it." Apr. 1, 1736. Fast-day sermon. Top of last digit is worn off, but paper and handwriting best fit 1736.

389. All That Natural Men Do Is Wrong (Romans 3:11–12). Apr. 1736.

390. Psalms 127:2. "That those that trust in themselves and seek satisfaction and rest for their souls in their own strength do commonly weary themselves with many vain attempts." May 1736.

391. Acts 4:11.* "Unbelievers set nothing by all the glory and excellency in Christ." May 1736. Published as Unbelievers Contemn the Glory and Excellency of Christ in Sermons (1780), pp. 57–71; repr. Worcester rev. ed., 4, 361–67.

393. James 2:18. "A manifestation of godliness in a man's life and walk is a better ground of others' charity concerning his godliness than any account that he gives about it in words." May 1736.

Whatever graces we think we see or feel in ourselves, that these graces be such [as] are effectual and fruitful." June 1736.

400. Deuteronomy 28:12. "The blessing of rain is a blessing that we are supplied with no otherwise than as God brings it forth out of his good treasure." July 1736. "Private meeting to pray for rain."

402. 2 Chronicles 25:9. "The way of duty is the way to prosperity." July 1736.

403. A City on a Hill (Matthew 5:14). July 1736.

404. Luke 16:8–9. "We ought to shew ourselves as wise for our spiritual and eternal interest as worldly minded men are wont to do for their temporal interest." July 1736.

439. Matthew 23:33. "'Tis in itself most rational to suppose that wicked men should be most extremely and eternally miserable in another world." July 1737.

440. Mark 8:38. "That Christ will hereafter treat those that have continued in unbelief under the gospel as he has been treated by 'em in this world." July 1737.
