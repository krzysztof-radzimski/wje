AFFECTIONS NOTEBOOK, "NO. 7"

One of a series of notebooks Edwards kept while composing Religious Affections, this is apparently the only one extant, though the stray leaves containing "Notes on Signs" in the Historical Society of Pennsylvania, along with the single leaf at Jonathan Edwards College, may have constituted part of another. There were at least nine books in the series, according to Edwards' own cross-references. The majority of the entries here are struck through, indicating Edwards used them in one place or another.

Edwards connected entries thematically by means of cue signs, though the passages similarly marked do not necessarily create a continuous discussion. In the edited version, therefore, the cues are left in place. Only on occasions where Edwards clearly meant to relocate a passage is his direction followed.
