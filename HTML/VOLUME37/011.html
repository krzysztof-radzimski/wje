Note To “Paper on Justification” (Notes on Imputation and Free Grace)

The title for this manuscript is Edwards’ own, as given in “On Free Will.” Possibly, its contents were notes for revisions to “Justification by Faith Alone” as Edwards prepared the discourse for publication. This conjecture is supported by dates of the documents used for the notes (“Justification by Faith Alone” was preached in Nov. 1734, and the notes are no earlier than Oct. 1734) as well as similar ink and hand. Parallels with the Justification discourse are provided in the annotations. Yet there are also significant portions that do not relate to the discourse. The manuscript is interesting for both these unused portions and for formative passages on justification, imputation, and grace.

With “On Free Will” and “Book of Minutes on the Arminian Controversy” (Gazeteer Notebook), this MS forms a phalanx of polemic pieces relating to Edwards’ anti-Arminian campaign. His unexplained references, such as “they,” “their scheme,” even simply “it,” refers to Arminian writers and their position on justification.

* * * * *

The manuscript is in two pieces. First is a broadside proclamation for a Massachusetts Thanksgiving, by Gov. Jonathan Belcher, dated Oct. 3, 1734 (printed in Boston). Edwards started on the blank side, dividing it into six columns (secs. 1-12), and continued on the printed side, dividing it into two columns that wrap around the printed text (secs. 13-18). The top and right (if the printed side is up) edges are badly tattered, resulting in lacunae. Some passages have a vertical, or “use,” line drawn through them. The second part of the manuscript is formed by two pieces of paper, stitched together.

-- --

One piece is an undated prescription for Sarah Edwards from Samuel Mather, Northampton’s physician. Edwards wrote secs. 19-20 on the blank side. The other is a letter from the Rev. Nehemiah Bull of Westfield, dated Nov. 4, 1734 (Letter B003). On the backside of this paper, folded unevenly, Edwards made two short columns of notes; however, their relation to the previous entries is uncertain.
