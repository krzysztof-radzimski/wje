"Treatise on Grace" (BRBL fragment) Treatise on Grace (BRBL frag.) Jonathan Edwards

"Treatise on Grace" Fragment

Most likely this is a fragment of a draft of the "Treatise on Grace," dating from the late 1730s or early 1740s. That the portions preserved here are on duodecimo-sized signatures strongly suggests that Edwards, as was his wont, preached the content of the treatise as a series of lectures before working it up in finished form.

*****

BRBL, f. 1253. Three duodecimo signatures, numbered by Edwards pp. 93-100, 117-20, the latter corresponding with the very end of the treatise. In the edited version, in Works, 21, the text corresponds to pp. 187-90, and pp. 196-97.
