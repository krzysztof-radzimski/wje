Appendix: Dated Sermons, January 1739–December 1742

-- 537 --

The following list records the extant sermons that Edwards preached from January 1739 through December 1742. Sermons are listed according to the original Schafer number (named for Thomas A. Schafer, who first arranged the sermons and put in chronological order the early, undated ones), followed by the title, if printed, or by the Scripture text, a statement of the doctrine, date of composition, and any other relevant information, if unpublished. Sermons delivered in the same month are listed in canonical order. In some cases, more precise dating of a sermon consequent to its original numbering may be noted in its description. Sermon texts followed by an asterisk are printed in previous editions. Unless stated otherwise, the manuscript is in the Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University.

499. Psalms 24:7-10. “Jesus Christ entering into his glory after his suffering was a sight worthy to be beheld with great admiration.” Jan. 1739.

500. Matthew 23:15. “Wicked men are the children of hell.” Jan. 1739.

501. Luke 4:38-39. “When persons are restored from dangerous sickness, their business should be to serve and minister to him that has restored them.” Jan. 1739.An unidentified epidemic hit Northampton in late 1738. In October, Ebenezer Hunt recorded in his diary that “Pelatiah Holbrook died after a sickness of 5 weeks, Oliver Warner was sick about 3 weeks ([Hunt's] Apprentices) & I was unable to work about a fortnight.” Judd MSS, v. 1, Mass. Series, “Abstracts & Extracts from Deac. Ebenezer Hunts Journal,” p. 24.

502. Luke 6:48. “They that would build an hope of heaven, well should dig deep and lay their foundation on a rock.” Jan. 1739.

503. Ephesians 1:10. “Jesus Christ is the great medium and head of union in whom all elect creatures in heaven and earth are united to God and to one another.” Originally two lectures.The MS is a fragment. Only two leaves remain of the first lecture; the second commences on the third leaf with the date, “Feb. 1738, 9, ” at the top.

-- 538 --

504. Genesis 3:11. “The act of our first father in eating the forbidden fruit was a very heinous act.” Feb. 1739. Four sermons.

505. Acts 9:13-15. “'Tis so with respect to God's bestowment of saving mercy on some persons, that there is no other account to be given of it but that he will show mercy.” Feb. 1739.

506. 1 John 3:15. “When a man is converted, eternal life is begun in him.” Feb. 1739. Repreached May 1755.

507. Psalms 66:18(a). “'Tis in vain for any to expect to have their prayers heard as long as they continue in the allowance of sin.” Mar. 29, 1739. Fast.

508. Isaiah 51:8. “The work of redemption is a work that God carries on from the fall of man to the end of the world.” A series of thirty sermons in twelve booklets. Mar.–Aug. 1739.First published in Edinburgh in 1774 under the title, A History of the Work of Redemption. See Works, 9.

509. Matthew 25:46. “The misery of the wicked in hell will be absolutely eternal.” Apr. 1739.

510. Christ the Spiritual Sun (Malachi 4:2). May 1739.

511. Proverbs 4:7. “An interest in Christ should be sought above all things in the world.” June 1739.

512. Acts 6:1-03. “The main business of a deacon, by Christ's appointment, is to take care of the distribution of the church's charity for the outward supply of those that need.” June 14, 1739.

513. Luke 1:77-79 (a). “How a sinner that has offended God should be reconciled to him again and restored to his favor, could be known no other way but by divine revelation.” July 1739, repreached Nov. 1756. Lecture delivered at New Haven.

514. The Means and Ends of Excommunication (1 Corinthians 5:11). * July 22, 1739, on the excommunication of Mrs. Bridgman. Dwight ed., 6, 569–80.

515. 2 Corinthians 8:5. “We ought to give our own selves to Jesus Christ, to yield and devote ourselves to God.” July 1739.

516. Romans 12:4-08. “The offices that Christ has appointed in his church, do respect either the souls or bodies of men.” Aug. 19, 1739, “On occasion of the ordination of the deacons.”The members installed that day as deacons were Stephen Wright, Noah Cook, and Ebenezer Pomeroy, Jr.

517. Psalms 19:12. “'Tis an exceeding hard thing for men to be sensible of those things in themselves that are sinful and offensive to God.” Sept. 1739.

518. Luke 9:61-62. “Looking back after persons have once engaged is

-- 539 --

especially disagreeable and mischievous in religion.” Sept. 1739.

519. John 1:47. “'Tis a great thing to be indeed a converted person.” Sept. 1739. Three preaching units.

520. Luke 11:21-22. “The conquering power of God gloriously appears in the saving work of his Spirit on the soul.” Oct. 1739, repreached 1750. Three preaching units.MS lacks two, possibly four, leaves at beginning.

521. Luke 22:44. “That which I shall make the subject of my present discourse, is Christ's agony.” Oct. 1739, repreached “winter and spring” 1757.

522. Job 38:7. “The angels of heaven do praise God together.” Nov. 29, 1739. Thanksgiving.

523. Proverbs 11:7 (3). “When a wicked man dies, all his hope and expectation perishes.” Nov. 1739.

524. Luke 8:2-03. “When Christ has delivered persons from the power of Satan, 'tis very becoming of them to show their love to Christ by ministering to him of their substance.” Nov. 1739. Thanksgiving.

525. The Importance and Advantage of a Thorough Knowledge of Divine Truth (Hebrews 5:12). * Nov. 1739. No MS extant; printed in Worcester rev. ed., 4, 1–15.

526. John 3:16. “There is an answerableness between the greatness of the misery of hell and the happiness of heaven, and the greatness of the things that have been done that sinners might be saved from hell and might obtain heaven.” Dec. 1739.

527. John 21:5. “Christ has a tender concern and love for his people, that they should be provided for and their wants supplied, as a father has for his children.” Dec. 1739.

528. Romans 8:29-30. “The things which God doth for the salvation and blessedness of the saints are like an inviolable chain reaching from a duration without beginning to a duration without end.” Dec. 7, 1739. Contribution lecture.

529. God's Grace Carried On in Other Places (2 Corinthians 8:1). Dec. 1739. Private meeting.

530. Mercy and Not Sacrifice (Matthew 12:7). Jan. 1740.

531. Luke 15:22. “A truly penitent sinner has the best righteousness that ever any creature appeared in.” Jan. 1740.

532. John 3:7. “'Tis no wonder that Christ said that we must be born again.” Jan. 1740.

533. John 10:37 (a). “The miracles that Christ wrought when he was here

-- 540 --

upon earth were divine works.” Jan. 30, 1740, repreached Dec. 1756. Lecture.

534. Hebrews 9:15-16. “The covenant of grace is as it were Christ's last will and testament.” June 1740, repreached Jan. 1753. Lecture.

535. Numbers 11:10-15. “I.… Moses' complaint of the great burden and trouble of that office and work which God had appointed him to, of leading the children of Israel through the wilderness to Canaan.” Feb. 1740.

536. Psalms 94:8-11. “There is an extreme and brutish blindness in things of religion that naturally possesses the hearts of mankind.” Feb. 1740. Three sermons.

537. Psalms 94:10. * “Those that are of God's own people have much more reason to fear God's chastisements for their sins than others.” Feb. 1740. Dwight ed., 7, 3–30.

538. Philippians 3:17. * “We ought to follow the good example of the apostle Paul.” Feb. 1740. Four sermons. Dwight ed., 8, 123–58.

539. 2 Chronicles 36:15-16. “'Tis not God's manner to destroy a covenant people for their backslidings, till they have so persisted in their backslidings under means used with them to reclaim 'em, that there seems [to] be no prospect of any remedy.” Mar. 27, 1740. Fast.

540. John 10:37-38 (b). “The miracles that Christ wrought when he was here upon earth were divine works.” Mar. 12, 1740. Lecture. Completion of no. 533.

541. Titus 2:14(a). “One main thing that Christ aimed at in what he did and suffered in the work of our redemption was to save men from sin.” Mar. 1740.

542. Matthew 14:12 (a). “When anyone is taken away by death that has been eminent in the work of the gospel ministry, 'tis suitable that such as are bereaved thereby should go and spread their sorrows before Jesus.” Apr. 14, 1740. “On occasion of the funeral of the Rev. Mr. [Nehemiah] Bull of Westfield [Mass.].”For a revision of this sermon that was published, see below, no. 634.

543. Zeal an Essential Virtue of a Christian (Titus 2:14 [b]). Apr. 1740.

544. Hebrews 12:22-24 (a). “I. I would show how Christians may be said to be come to Mt. Zion.” Apr. 1740.

545. Hebrews 12:22-24 (b). “I come now, in the second place, to show how Christians are come to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem.… I come now, in the third place, to show [how] Christians are come to an innumerable company of angels.” N.d. [Apr. 1740].

-- 541 --

546. Hebrews 12:22-24(c). “I come now to show, in the fourth place, how Christians are said to come to the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven.” Apr. 1740. MS defective at end.

547. Hebrews 12:22-24 (d). “We come now to the fifth particular in the text, to show how Christians are come to God, the judge of all.” N.d. [c. Apr.–May 1740].

548. Hebrews 12:22-24 (e). “Sixth particular… how Christians are come to the spirits of just men made perfect.” N.d. [c. Apr.–May 1740].

549. Hebrews 12:22-24 (f). “Seventh particular… [and unto Jesus, the mediator of the new covenant].” N.d. [c. Apr.–May 1740].

550. Hebrews 12:22-24 (g). “Eighth and last particular… how Christians are come to the blood of sprinkling that speaketh better things than the blood of Abel.” N.d. [c. Apr.–May 1740].

551. Romans 11:7 (a). “There are multitudes of men that seek salvation that never obtain what they seek for.” May 1740, repreached Aug. 1753 to Stockbridge Indians.

552. Romans 11:7 (b). “Though a people that live under means are wont in general to seek and hope for salvation, yet 'tis the election only that obtains, and the rest are blinded.” May 1740.

553. 1 Corinthians 2:11-13. * “Ministers are not to preach those things which their own wisdom or reason suggests, but the things that are already dictated to them by the superior wisdom and knowledge of God.” May 7, 1740. Ordination sermon for Edward Billing at Cold Spring, Mass. Southern Baptist Journal of Theology 3 (1999), 16–33.

554. Job 27:10. * “However hypocrites may continue for a season in the duty of prayer, yet 'tis their manner after a while in a great measure to leave it off.” June 1740. Dwight ed., 6, 69–88.

555. Luke 10:42 (b). “That which God gives to the saints for their part, is the best good that ever any of the children of men obtain.” June 1740, repreached Sept. 1754 to the Stockbridge Indians.

556. Romans 11:10. “Some men are laid under such a curse that they are forever bowed down to the earth and never are able to lift up themselves.” June 1740.

557. Exodus 20:15. * “I… shall make the command itself, as the words of it [‘Thou shalt not steal’] plainly lie before us in the Decalogue, my subject.”This sermon had its origins in an entry in Sermon Notebook “45.” MS p. 13, where JE reminds himself “To preach a sermon against robbing fruit trees and gardens, etc., before fruit time, if I should live.” Dwight ed., 6, 517–35.

-- 542 --

558. John 3:10-11. * “There is such a thing as conversion.” July 1740. The Sermons of Jonathan Edwards: A Reader, 82–104.

559. Romans 14:7-09. “'Tis the peculiar excellency of the gospel of Jesus Christ, that it teaches men how to live well and how to die well.” July 1740.

560. The Danger of Corrupt Communication Among Young People (Ephesians 4:20). July 1740. “For a meeting of young people.”

561. 1 Thessalonians 5:23. “In true conversion, men's bodies are in some respect changed as well as their souls.” July 1740.

562. Revelation 3:12. “I would show how those that are thorough Christians are properly deciphered by that, that they are they that overcome.” July 1740, repreached Aug. 1752. Lecture.

563. Children Ought to Love the Lord Jesus Christ Above All (Matthew 10:37 [a]). Aug. 1740. “To the children.”

564. The Subjects of a First Work of Grace May Need a New Conversion (Luke 22:32). Aug. 1740.

565. Revelation 7:17. “The shepherd that leads and feeds the church of saints like a flock is himself a lamb.” Aug. 1740, repreached Dec. 1757.

566. Genesis 6:22. * “We should be willing to engage in and go through with that which is a great undertaking in order to our own salvation.” Sept. 174[0]. Dwight ed., 6, 5–22.

567. Job 21:22-26. “God exercises perfect wisdom in ordering things as he does with respect to the death of mankind.” Sept. 1740. Possibly a funeral sermon.

568. Psalms 119:56. “When persons have strictly adhered to their duty under temptation and opposition, they commonly afterwards in this world find that benefit and reward of it that makes glad they have done so.” Sept. 1740.Probably preached on the celebrated occasion of the visit to Massachusetts of Eunice Williams, the “unredeemed captive,” a cousin of JE's who was taken by Indians in 1704. At the end of the sermon JE writes “Journey,” perhaps referring to a trip he took or intended to take to Longmeadow, where Williams and her Indian husband were staying at the home of her brother, the Rev. Stephen Williams.

569. Isaiah 26:10-11. “The obstinacy of some wicked men is such that means won't win them, nor will all the advantages enjoyed in God's visible church take any effect upon them, nor will any of God's providential judgments or threatenings reclaim them.” Sept. 1740.

570. Matthew 5:20. “I. Show how far the Pharisees went in religion, and II. How our righteousness must exceed theirs if we would ever enter into the kingdom of heaven.” Oct. 1740, repreached Jan. 1753. Lecture. MS at Amherst College, Amherst, Mass.

-- 543 --

571. Job 19:25. * “It is matter of great comfort and rejoicing to anyone in whatever circumstances he is in, when he can say that he knows his redeemer lives.” Oct. 1740, repreached Dec. 1754 and Jan. 1755 to the Stockbridge Indians. The Sermons of Jonathan Edwards: A Reader, 141–60.

572. John 6:45. “There never was any man that once came to understand what manner of one Christ was but his heart was infallibly drawn to him.” Oct. 1740.

573. Gospel Ministers a Savor of Life or of Death (2 Corinthians 2:15-16).

574. Matthew 13:3-04. “Those that God sends forth to preach the gospel are fitly compared to an husbandman that goes forth to sow his seed.” Nov. 1740, repreached April 1756. First of six sermons on the parable of the sower.

575. Matthew 13:3-04. “I would show how that there are some kinds of hearers of the word preached whose hearts are like the wayside.” [Nov.] 1740, repreached May 1756. Second of six sermons on the parable of the sower.

576. Matthew 13:5-06. “There are many of the hearers of the word whose hearts are like a rock with a thin covering of earth.” Nov. 1740, repreached May 1756. Third of six sermons on the parable of the sower.

577. Matthew 13:5-06. “Sudden conversions are very often false.” Nov. 1740. Fourth of six sermons on the parable of the sower.

578. Matthew 13:5-06. “That religion that arises only from superficial impressions is wont to wither away for want of root when it comes to be tried by the difficulties of religion.” [Nov. 1740.] Fifth of six sermons on the parable of the sower.

579. Matthew 13:7. “I. That the hearts of some of the hearers of the word are so carnal and worldly that they appear to be aground that was never plowed and so is overgrown with thorns.” Nov. 1740, repreached May 1756. Final of six sermons on the parable of the sower.

580. Psalms 144:15. “The interest of a people mainly consists in the interest of religion among them.” Nov. 1740. Private meeting.The MS has a separately stitched Application preached to an unidentified church that was in the process of choosing a minister (probably the church of Hadley, Mass.; see next note).

581. Isaiah 52:3. “I.… We have sold ourselves for nought.… II. Those that come to Christ are redeemed without money.” Nov. 1740.

582. Praying for the Spirit (Luke 11:13). Nov. 1740. Fast.

583. They Sing a New Song (Revelation 14:3). Nov. 1740. Thanksgiving.

584. Bringing the Ark to Zion a Second Time (1 Chronicles 15:12-13). Dec. 1740.

-- 544 --

585. Sinners in Zion (Isaiah 33:14 [a]). Dec. 1740.

586. Seeking After Christ (Matthew 2:10). Dec. 1740.

587. Acts 10:4-06. * “To be much in deeds of charity is the way to have spiritual discoveries.” [Jan. or Feb.] 1741. The Sermons of Jonathan Edwards: A Reader, 197–211.

588. Job 21:19-20. “Wicked men have no reason to doubt the truth of anything that is said in the Word of God, concerning the future punishment of the ungodly, or to suspect whether it be true.” Jan. 1741.

589. Like Rain upon Mown Grass (Psalms 72:6). Jan. 1741. Sacrament.

590. Matthew 7:13-14 (a). “I. I would speak to those things that are here asserted of the way that leads to destruction.… [II.] I proceed to consider things that are asserted concerning the way that leads to life.” Jan. 1741. Private meeting.

591. Acts 14:23. “The solemn setting apart of one to the work and office of a gospel minister is an affair wherein God should earnestly be sought.” Jan. 1741. “Fast at Hadley preceding ordination.”For the ordination of Chester Williams, which took place at Hadley, Mass., on Jan. 21, 1741

592. 2 Kings 2:23-24. “God is very angry at the sins of children.” Feb. 1741. “To the children at a private meeting.”

593. Youth Is Like a Flower That Is Cut Down (Job 14:2). Feb. 1741. Funeral.

594. Hosea 11:3. “The affair of seeking salvation is an affair wherein persons do exceedingly need God's help.” Feb. 17, 1741.

595. Matthew 11:12 (b). “Persons seeking heaven should behave in like manner as valiant resolute soldiers do in taking a country or kingdom in which they are strongly opposed.” Three sermons. Feb. 1741, repreached in part in 1752 to Stockbridge Indians, and in 1753 in Boston.An earlier repreaching of this sermon, not represented among JE's notations on the MS itself, was on April 15, 1741, at the weekly lecture of the Second Church of Hartford, Conn., where the Rev. Daniel Wadsworth heard him and recorded the event in his Diary (Hartford, 1894), 63.

596. God's Care for His Servants in Time of Public Calamities (Revelation 7:1-03). Feb. 26, 1741. “Fast for success in the war.”

597. Isaiah 40:20-31 (a). “Obs. 1. Those that have the greatest strength have no strength of their own sufficient to carry 'em on in that way which God calls 'em to travel.” Mar. 1741. First of series of three booklets, containing Obs. 1–3.

598. Isaiah 40:20-31 (b). “Obs. 4. The degrees in which God gives strength and grace to those that wait upon him are very diverse.” [Mar. 1741.] Second of series of three booklets, containing Obs. 4–5.

599. Isaiah 40:20-31 (c). “Obs. 6.… To wait on God in the improvement of

-- 545 --

what strength we have is the way to receive more and more strength.” Mar. 29, 1741. Last in series of three booklets on this text, containing Obs. 6–7.

600. 2 Kings 7:2. “Some persons are the subjects of such a judgment of God, that though they live to see a glorious plenty after a famine that they may see others partake of, yet they themselves never have one taste of it.” Mar. 1741. “Contribution lecture.”

601. Psalms 27:4 (a). “Earnest desires and strong resolutions [and humble dependence]Bracketed phrase added by JE for later preaching date. should accompany one another in seeking spiritual and eternal good.” Mar. 1741. Private meeting. Repreached July 1754.

602. Jeremiah 8:7. “They that don't observe and improve their special seasons for the good of their souls, are less observant of what is for their own preservation and comfort than the very fowls of the air.” Mar. 1741. “Private meeting at Pascommuck.” Incomplete at end.

603. Revelation 3:7. “I. I would show how Christ is said to have the key of David.… II. How when he opens, no man shutteth.… III. How when he shuts, no man opens.” Mar. 1741. Lecture. Repreached in April 1752, and at Sheffield [?], Nov. 1753; Boston, Oct. 1753; and Elizabethtown at an unspecified date.

604. Genesis 27:39. “Those that God hates, he is, notwithstanding, oftentimes willing to give 'em a-plenty of these earthly things, and the choicest of them.” Apr. 1741.

605. Importunate Prayer for Millennial Glory (Isaiah 62:6-07). Apr. 1741. Fast.

606. Ezekiel 22:14 (a). * “Since God has undertaken to deal with impenitent sinners, they shall neither shun the threatened misery nor deliver themselves out of it, nor can they bear it.” Apr. 1741, repreached Feb. 1755. Dwight ed., 6, 89–96.

607. Luke 18:38-39. “Sinners that meet with opposition and discouragements in their seeking mercy, should be as it were willful and obstinate in it that they will seek mercy.” Apr. 1741.

608. Zechariah 12:10. “When that wonderful time of the outpouring of the Spirit of God comes that will introduce the glorious times of the church on earth, it will operate in a remarkable manner as a Spirit of prayer.” Apr. 1741. Private meeting.

609. John 5:45. “The law which natural men trust in to justify them will only condemn 'em.” Apr. 1741.

610. Isaiah 61:2. “A time of great pouring out of the Spirit is always a time of

-- 546 --

remarkable favor to some and of extraordinary vengeance on others.” May 1741.

611. Mary's Remarkable Act (Mark 14:3). May 1741.

612. Luke 19:41. “To give the reasons why Christ wept over Jerusalem.… I. He knew that the greater part of the inhabitants were in a graceless unconverted state.… II. He knew the greatness of the misery of such a state.… III. Christ not only knew that most of the inhabitants of Jerusalem were not only subjects of the common misery of unconverted sinners, but that the sinners of that city were in a distinguishingly deplorable state.” May 1741, repreached in 1751 at an unspecified place, and also at Cold Spring, Springfield, Stockbridge, Boston, Ipswich Hamlet, and Canaan.

613. Revelation 22:16-17. “Both the bridegroom and the bride do, with united voice, freely and earnestly invite poor sinners to the entertainments of the wedding of the Lamb.” May 1741, repreached March 1753. Two booklets, once sewn together. Defective at end.

614. Sinners in the Hands of an Angry God (Deuteronomy 32:35 [c]). June 1741, repreached July 8, 1741, at Enfield, Mass., and at other unspecified places.JE may have begun repreaching this almost immediately after the success at Enfield. On Aug. 1, 1741, Stephen Williams of Longmeadow recorded that JE had preached at Hadley that morning “& yt ye Effect was wonderful as at Enfield.”

615. Psalms 13:3. “I. I would show when the souls of persons may be said to be asleep.… II. How that there are some persons that do sleep the sleep of death.” June 1741.

616. Mark 2:17. “What induces Christ to pity and help sinners, is not that they deserve it but that they need it.” June 1741.

617. Luke 19:10(a). “I would show, I. Whence Christ came.… II. Whither he came.… III. Those that he came for the sake of, were poor lost creatures.… IV. That he came to seek them.… V. That he seeks them to that end, that he may save them.” June 1741.

618. Luke 19:42 (b). “I. If ever those that are in a special manner far from conversion are converted, 'tis most likely to be at a time when Christ is extraordinarily present.” June 1741.

619. Romans 13:11. “I. How persons may be said to be spiritually asleep.… II. Upon what accounts it may be said of some, that it is high time for 'em to awake out of sleep.” June 1741.

620. The Blowing of the Great Trumpet (Isaiah 27:13). June 1741.

621. Deuteronomy 32:23. “‘I will heap mischiefs upon them; I will spend mine arrows upon them.’” July 1741.

-- 547 --

622. Psalms 34:11. “Therefore now I would improve this scripture to call upon you children now to forsake your sins and seek the fear of God.” July 1741. “Private meeting of children.”

623. John 1:10-11. “I shall show how the case was with them with respect to Christ's appearance among them in those days, and their not knowing or receiving him; and 2. shall make application of these observations to our own case, or the case of God's professing people in these days, with respect to the coming of Christ that now is or is soon to be expected.” July 1741.

624. Acts 8:23. “I. I shall show what is the proper meaning of the phrase [‘in the gall of bitterness’]. II. How the phrase is applicable to all unconverted persons.” July 1741.

Undated Sermons

693a. Genesis 19:17. “'Tis needful for those that seek an escape from God's wrath to avoid these things: lingering, looking back and trusting in anything short of the true refuge.” C. 1741. According to Ebenezer Parkman's diary, Edwards preached this sermon at Westborough, Feb. 2, 1742 (Tracy, Great Awakening, 204).

693b. Genesis 25:29-34. Theme: Esau selling his birthright. C. 1741–42. MS at Andover Newton Theological School.

693c. Psalms 1:3 (a). “Christ is to the heart of a saint like a river to the root of a tree that is planted by it.” C. 1742. MS at Andover Newton Theological School.

693d. Psalms 18:25. No MS. Ebenezer Parkman records in his diary that JE preached on this text at Grafton or Sutton, Feb. 1, 1742 (Tracy, Great Awakening, 204).

693e. Proverbs 27:1. “We ought to behave ourselves every day as though we had no dependence on any other day.” C. 1739–41.

693f. John 12:23. No MS. According to Parkman's diary, JE preached on this text at Leicester, Jan. 28, 1742 (Tracy, Great Awakening, 204).

693g. John 12:32. Three MS fragments [c. 1739–41], apparently parts of an Application for one sermon, rewritten for repreaching, or parts of Applications for more than one sermon. The first contains Uses I–III, beginning, “I. Call upon and exhort all that are in a natural condition now at this time to turn from their sins to God.” The second fragment contains another Use III: “I would apply myself to such as have an interest in the Lord Jesus Christ.” Fragment three, dating from the fall or winter of 1739–40, contains the last part of a first Use; p. 8 begins “2dly. Consider how excellent a state you are invited to.” Parkman states that JE preached on this text at Westborough, Feb. 2, 1742 (Tracy, Great Awakening, 204).

693h. Hebrews 2:3. “They that neglect so great salvation as is offered in the gospel, do bring themselves into exceeding danger of damnation.” No MS; copy at Andover Newton Theological School.
