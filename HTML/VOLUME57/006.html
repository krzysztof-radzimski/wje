____that we endeavour to be und as Gods People und

in those times will be. & to behave as

Gods people will then behave Time of Great Light

______

Then be Eminently Holy. Let us seek.

______

Then Remarkeable a time of Peace & Love

___________________________________________________

2 Le that we do what we can to promote

things that have a tendency.

G. will make use of men of the Ins his

Chh. They shall be I the Instruments

____

to promote Relig. in families.

_____

among neighbours. Example.

_________

all set to with heart & head to promote

a general Conve Reviving of Relig. in this

Town. among the Godly

______

Xs armies following him.

______

Help of the L. against the mighty. Judg. 5. 23.
