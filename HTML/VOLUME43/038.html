I answer there is no need of it upon these two accounts

___________

1. There is an Encouragement a person may take from

his own striving which is not selfrighteousness as we

have shown you already. because there is Encouragement

Given in Gods word & Providence. by both we are taught

thus much that this is Gods ordinary way wherin he

bestows saving Grace. so that he very often does bestow

it upon such and ordinarily upon no others tho he has

not Promised that he will bestow it upon all such

2. if you were brought to see Gods sovereignty you [L. 23r.]

would see that he might show you mercy if he Pleased

and as well as that he might not if Refuse if he

Pleased. and that will Raise a hope in you to see

that God may show you mercy if he Pleases. self right-

eous men bent Convinced fully Convinced of neither

They are neither bent Convinced that God may Refuse

them mercy It seems to them very hard that he should

utterly Cast them down to hell and that he should have

no Regard to their Good works. nor yet be they Con-

vinced that God may show them mercy they sometimes

are Ready to Argue with themselves that they are so Great

sinners God never will show mercy to them. they are afraid

there is no hopes for them they have affronted God so much

they fear he is unreconcileable they think his honour Requi

Requires that they should be Punished . .

and thus they are tossd between two Opinions their

hearts are all in a tumult sometimes thinking that ought

to show them mercy and that it would be very hard if

he dont and other times almost despairs arguing

that they are such Great sinners that there is no hopes

_________________

But If you saw Gods sovereignty then you would Come to

an Conclusion that God might do as he Pleases y & you

see Plain that he might show you mercy if he Pleased and

this would Raise a hope and would Ease your mind of

y Burthen & Put an and to those tumults which now

your wavering hesitating doubtfull mind is in

ought
