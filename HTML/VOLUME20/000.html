FUTURE STATE. 1136. INFINITE EVIL OF SIN. 1137. HAPPINESS OF HEAVEN. When God had finished the work of creation, he is represented as resting and being refreshed and rejoicing in his works. The Apostle compares the happiness Christ entered into, after he had finished his labors and sufferings in the work of redemption, to this ( Hebrews 4:5 , he points out that "In the expression of MY REST, the Apostle plainly understands… the rest that God himself enjoys." The Apostle therefore, according to JE, is here exhorting Christians "to seek to be partakers of the rest that Christ enjoys, that he entered into when he finished and ceased from his works." this seems to argue two things: viz. 1. that the way that the saints will be happy in the beholding the glory of God will be very much in beholding the glory of his perfections in his works; 2. that the happiness of the saints in heaven, especially since Christ's ascension, consisting in beholding God's glory, will consist very much in seeing his glory in the work of redemption. The happiness of departed saints under the old testament consisted much in beholding the glory of God in the works of creation, in the beholding which the morning stars sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy. But their happiness since Christ's ascension consists much more in beholding the glory of God in the work of redemption, since that the old creation in comparison of this is no more mentioned nor comes into mind, but they will be glad and rejoice forever in this work. The beatifical vision of God in heaven consists mostly in beholding the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ, either in his work or in his person as appearing in the glorified human nature.
