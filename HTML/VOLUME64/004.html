4. After the fall of Louisburg, an epidemic broke out among the English; shortly after the capitulation, nearly 1500 men were down with fever and dysentery, and by the spring of 1746 nearly 900 occupying English had died. ↩
5. Perhaps referring, among other reasons for discontent, to the reneged-upon promise of booty to New Englanders who volunteered for the Cape Breton campaign. ↩
