3. This is a list of the entries on the verses of the book of Revelation written by JE in the "Blank Bible." The parentheses indicate multiple notes on a given text. The asterisks point to cross-references on the margins of the small printed Bible. No attempt has been made to indicate the date of the entries or the variations in length and content. The more than ninety cross-references to the AP appearing in the "Blank Bible" are not listed here. ↩
