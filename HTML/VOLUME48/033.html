there is opportunity to Escape & G. is using many

means with you to that with you to Recover

you. Consider this that as yet You

bent so Left in the hands of this your Master marginal

& Enemy that there is no hope of Escape

X has Come into the T to on purpose to spoil

Princip. ---------- to destroy the works of

the devils. & deliver Poor Captivated souls

& is Calling to you. & there are outward means

the word & ordinances . & there are the strivings

of the sp. of G. that is wont to accompany

the outw. means. now You have a fair

Opp. of Escape wh w but all these things

you will be wholly deprived of at death.
