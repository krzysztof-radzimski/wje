Earthly minded men have this

to view all their outward Prosperity marginal

if they Possess finte Legion Great farms .

& or he Chests full of silver & Gold. or

see thems. at the Head of Great & Populous

Kingdoms they may take a Great deal of

Pleasure in viewing their Lands & in hand

Looking into their Chests of silver & Gold.

& seeing the Honour they are advanced to

but whom all after all there is this in men

all their Pleasure that they must Leave

in all in a Little time.

________________________

But there will be no such thing to be

a damp to the joy Joy of the saints in th the

Enjoymt. of X nor will there be any ground

for it but they shall not only not fear that

their Happiness will Ever Come to an End but

they will know that it never will. & the

thought of the Eternity of it will Crown all

& will make Heaven Heaven indeed. it will

be be the Glory of their Glory.
