B6. Letter from Aaron Burr, March 1741 B006.A.Burr-JE 3-41 edited Aaron Burr

B006. Aaron Burr to JE, 3/41. ANTS, f. 1741, #7. Text lacking at end.

Newark March 1741

Revd & dear Sr

Your’s of Decr 14 Came Safe to hand ye 5th of this instant. I received it with Joy & heartily thank you for it. I desire to bless God that he inclined you to write & especialy to write So freely of your own Experiences. I think it has been much blessed to your my Spiritual Good: tho I have often heard & read of others Experiences, I neer with’m, any thing that the like Effect upon Me - It came in a most Seasonable Time, & was the Means of Clearing up Several Things that I was in ye dark about.

The Week that I received your Letter I was much in ye dark about my own State, And had determin’d by the first Opportunity to open my Case to you by a Letter, that I might have your Advice & Direction, which I Shall now do with ye greater Freedom Since I have heard from you. I think at present, I feel no Disposition to flatter My Self. I long to See & know the worst. The Thoughts of being deceived lie with great Weight on My Mind - I Shall endeavour to Speak of what God has done for [my] Soul without Reserve, according to the best Recollection I can Make of it, and I doubt not you will act the Part of a Spiritual Father & faithful Friend in your Reflections upon it.

From my Childhood I used to be under Distress of mind, great Terrours & horrors from a guilty Conscience and the Fears of Hell, at these Times, as I remember, I used to get

Relief by Promises & Resolutions, Especialy by reading

Bp. Bevearidges Thoughts on Religion.1 I used (when my Distress was great) to Set my Self as twere in ye Presence of God & Make over his Resolutions, which would give Me Ease & make Me more Strict in Secret Prayer for a Season, then would all wear off & I became as vain, & Careless as ever. Then I continued under frequent Strivings of God’s Spirit, which I refuted & Stifled, till I Commenced A:B. in ye year 1735 After this I reason’d my Self into a more thorough Reformation; forsook every thing But what ye Devil & my own deceitfull Heart flatered Me was right. Set up the Practice of all outward Duties & Soon began to be well pleased with My Self. By reading Foster, Tillotson, Sharp, &c I had found Such a rational Religion (as I then Called it) as afforded Me no Small Delight Such as ’twas. I thought there were few Such Christians to be found. When I used to hear of Ministers Speaking Against the Arminian Scheme, I used to pity their Ignorance. But O! the Pride that then reign’d in my Heart; it was the Secret Spring of all my Actions, whether I had to do with God or Man. All this Time I never see the Badness of my own Heart, and never Imagin’d but that I was much better than the Most of those among whom I conversed. And I Secretly rejoiced that others were worse than I, for marginal the Comparison used to Afford Me Peace & Comfort.

This continued not long before it pleased God to open My Eyes & Shew Me Something what I was I See my Self an holy God to be exceeding vile & odious in ye Sight of [-] I had a distressing Apprehension of my Misery & Danger, of that Eternal Wrath to which I lay exposed. I beheld Such a Fountain of Sin & Corruption, as I never imagin’d was in the Heart of any Man - I Soon Changed My Thoughts of being better than others & concluded that there was not Such a another Sinner on the Face of God’s Earth. I was much inclined to despairing Thoughts of God’s Mercy from a Sense of my own vileness; was much afraid I had Sinned away the Day of God’s Patience - I used to think, if I cou’d but be convinced that there ever was So great a Sinner Saved it would afford Me much Relief This Distress was much increased by an Apprehension, that I Should die in a little Time; (for this Winter I did Sensibly decay in my bodily Health) which Made often fear my Concern proceeded more from that than a Sense of Sin. And here I cant but use your own words, they do so exactly express the Sense I then & Since have had of my Case: that the greatness of my Distress was not owing to the greatness of my Convictions, but ye Degree of my Wickedness2 I did then & do Still think there never was Such a botomless Depth of Pride & Hypocrisy, Such an amazing Scene of ye most vile & abominable Thoughts indulged in the Heart of any Sinner as there was in mine.

At this Time I had no Just Notion in my Head about the Nature of Conviction, Conversion, & the Way of Salvation by Faith in Christ. There was no Suitable Care taken to instruct in those things in my early Days; I had Scarce ever heard any Conversation about them; learnt Nothing of them at Colledge. I never open’d My Case to any one, for I did not imagine any Body had ever been So before. at this Time as I remember I had Strong Desires after Christ wc I am convinced proceeded from the Fear of Hell. I did not question My Willingness to be Saved by him on any Terms; but Secretly his willingness to Save Me - I thought I could have laid down my Life or forsake 10000 Worlds If [I] had them for an Interest in Him - I dont remember I had any particular Thoughts about my unwillingness to come to Christ - I See that my Heart was fill’d with an Opposition to everything that was good and knew it must be Changed by the Almighty Power of divine Grace or I cou’d never be Saved.

I think I had a Sense of being in the Hands of a Sovereign God. That if he Sent Me to Hell & Saved others, I must pronounce him Just with my last Breath, That it must be by free Sovereign Grace if I ever was Saved - This Concern continued greater or less at Seasons, the most of that Winter I lived Bachelor at Colledge - One Morning in my Study as I was reading that Sermon of Dr. Watt’s on those Words of Job. O! that I knew where I might find him &c3 which Made a great & unusual Impression on my Mind in my S.I.4 Devotions afterwards I Seem’d to be near to God & have Liberty to pour out My Complaints before Him. My Sins Seemed to come fresh in my Mind in an unusual Maner.

And while I was confessing my original & actual Sins, marginal I felt an inward Melting & mourning that I never felt before & in my Prayer I had Such a Sense given Me of the Fullness & Sufficiency of the Lord Jesus Christ to Save the Chief of Sinners; I think it came from one of those two passages of Sacred Write viz. He is able to Save to the uttermost &c or That, His Blood Cleanseth from all Sin.5 I remember I then used that Argument with Some inward Satisfaction & Delight; Lord thou canst get Glory to thy gr Name in Saving Such a great Sinner as I am. I thought I See how the Justice of God could be glorified in my Salvation. -- upon this I felt Some inward Peace & Ease But I did not think then of being converted, or having Closed with Christ. -- Thou when I came to understand more of the Nature of these Things by reading I used to hope I was then brought home. About this Time I meet with a very considerable Change in my Principals, from an Arminian; became a Calvinist; though I not so any thing so Clear a view of the Calvinistical Doctrine as I have had Since. I used often to be puzzled with many Dificulties about them, tho I Seemed to have an inward Sense of those Truths; & thought they must be So whether I cou’d answer Objections made against them or not - I used to meet with Many Objections against the Sovereignty of God. But I found Something within that acquiesced in it; Saying Who art thou that replyest6 &c Tis the Lord let him do as he pleases. I used to live between Hopes & Fears. I often with Something of the Same Nature that I felt that Morning. - I used to feel Some Steady Concern upon My Mind about these Things. Sometimes in great Distress (but never felt such kind of Convictions as I had before tho I often Sought them). What used to be the greatest Support of hope was a Steady Desire, as I imagin’d I had after Christ, & of being like to Him . a willingness to Suffer the Loss of all Things for Him; tho my Practice did not Stand it. The Fall before last Sometime in Novr: in my return from NewEngland The Thoughts about my everlasting Peace & wellfare Came into my Mind with more power than usual, I reflected with Shame upon my living at Such a miserable low Rate as I had done, want of Zeal & Fidelity in my great Work. I began to fear all was wrong. That I [had] been building My Hopes on the Sand. - On my Return to My People, I providentially Meet with Mr. Law’s Treatise on Christian Perfection7; It made Such an Impression on My Mind that I can’t well Express.

What he Said Concerning renouncing the World and SelfDenial; which I Supposed all God’s Children complied with: So far Exceeded what ever I have Experienced that I concluded I knew Nothing yet as I ought to know. marginal The Foundation of all my hope failed Me. I resign’d up All my former Experiences for meer Delusions. Endeavoured to lay My Self at ye Footstool of Sovereign Grace; waiting for the Consolation of Israel. - The Concern I then felt was increased by what I heard of Mr. Whitefield’s Zeal & Success.

When I reflected on my past Life, I thought I See many Things inconsistent with a State of Grace. -- As my being So much Conform’d to this present World; being govern’d So much by worldly Hopes & Fears and thereby often hindred from dealing So plainly & faithfully with my People as I ought. - Finding So much Pride in My Heart, being often Secretly pleased with the vain Applause of my fellowWorms & greived with their Reproaches & Revilings - Finding So much Selfrighteous & Selfpleasing in my Duty. living So much on my Frames, as it appeared to me I had done, tho I then in the Time of it I thought I hated these Things. My being no more Zealous & faithfull in my great Work. - These Things made Me Conclude I had been deceiving my Self with a false Hope & false Confidence. - That Self had been at the Botom of all. -- In this State of Darkness I continued for Some Time; tho I did not feel any Thing as I did at Colledge had none of those Terrors & fearful Apprehensions of divine Wrath. - I found an engagedness of Spirit after the Things of my Peace. I thought little about being delivered from Hell. But I thought I longed exceedingly to be freed from all Sin, Especialy Pride, Selfrighteousness and a love to this present World. I Seem’d to loathe & hate my Self for that Sin I felt within; and longed to have Christ form’d in my Soul, to find [] holy, heavenly, divine Temper & Disposition - I often thought of what an eminent divine Says - I had rather feel the real Impression of a Godlike Nature on my Soul, than have a Vision from Heaven, or an Angel Sent to tell Me My Name was enroll’d in the Book of Life. - I thought to be free from all Sin, to be like God & Christ, would be Such an Heaven as I wanted. - I long’d for Such a Discovery of Christ to My Soul as would Change & transform Me into ye Same Image. - I devoted My Self to the Service of God, both in Secret & publick in another Manner than ever I had done before & with more pleasure & Delight. But dare not conclude I was in a Safe State. - About this Time one Day in my Secret Devotions: Christ Seemed to appear exceeding precious to my Soul, Methought, I did Commit my Soul to him, to be Saved in his Way. That I cou’d Spend & be Spent for him, part with all, take up my Cross & follow him - This gave Me Some Joy and Comfort, removed my Darkness in Some Measures. I found hereupon Such a Zeal for God’s Glory, Such a Love for Christ & Souls, as I never felt before. […]8
