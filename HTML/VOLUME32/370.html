B111. Letter from Ephraim Williams and "Aggrieved Brethren of Stockbridge Church, April 6, 1754 B111.E.Williams-JE 4-6-54 edited Ephraim Williams and "Aggrieved Brethren of Stockbridge (Mass.) Church

B111. Ephraim Williams et al to JE and Stockbridge Church, 4/6/54. RIHS.

To The Revd Mr Jonathan Edwards Pastor of the Chh in Stockbridge to be Communicated to the Said Chh -

Revd Sr It was with Some Surprise, we read your message of November 19th, your Severe Resentments at Some Expressions used by us in our remonstrance; and what (we must Confess) added to our Surprise, and in Some measure Interrupted our Serious Consideration; was our finding the Revd Pastor & Chh had fallen Into the Same Error (If it be an Error) they were Condemning in us (viz) Suggesting to us, that we have been guilty of Grossly abusing abus the Chh of Stockbridge; and that we are Chargable with the most Glaring absurdity; and that upon ye foundation of Such a Glaring absurdity, we go on to treat the Church In a reflecting & reproachfull manner; Stiling our reasoning Taunting Language; and again your Suggesting to us that we treat ye Church in an assuming manner; Interspersing here & there, in Invidious & Supercilious Language, Censorious Suggestions & insinuations. Might we not Justly think Sr that whilst your were reprimanding us, for Expressions which were harsh; that you would have been Cauteous of avoiding the unhappy mistake: for we Cant but think those Sacred texts; Rom 2:1 I:21: have Some relation to the Conduct of ministers & Chhs, as well as others - but least you think we would Excuse our selves upon this foot; we frankly own, it is no Excuse for us, If we have been Criminal, that you have been so too; tho’ we do not at present Determin, that Either of us have been so - we therefore now proceed to offer a few a few things, in Vindication of our selves, in using the Expressions you Except against; and with respect to them we Say; we don’t Charge them upon the Pastor, and Chh, any further then the principle they Seemed to us, by their votes to Set up oblidged us to - we had Signified our Dissatisfaction with The Pastor and Chh; and had desired that our Grievances, might be heard and and Decided by an Ecclesiastical Council; the Chh refused to gratifie us, and by their votes as appeared to us, Claimed a Prerogative, of Determining the Cause Themselves. It its true true they offered us, to Refer the Consideration of our Grievance, to the Honble Commissioners but at the Same time, Signified to us, that they had a right to Judge the Matter themselves.

And at least Intimated to us, that they would, unless we Complied with marginal Their proposal of referring to the Commiss Commissioners; This Was Excepted against to us matter of offence; we Except therefore against both the priniciple and proposal, & Supposed we had a right to do So. But Especially against the Chh’s right, to Set as Judges, it being their own Case; we argued upon this point only, to Shew & Expose the Nakedness & absurdity of pretending to Such a Right. We Humbly Repesented what face the Setting up Such a principle Reflected up upon our minds; and Could not but think, others would view it in ye Same light with us - but now Sr If we are mistaken and the Chh By their votes do not Claim Such authority; as you Seem to Intimate in your written message that the [ ]: our Controversy is so far at an End with them, for our observations and arguings were all founded (as is manifest) upon the Supposition, that the Chh had by their latter of their votes Claimed Such power which[?] you Say Sr to Shew, that the Chh did not Claim Such a power, Shall be perticularly Examined by & by.

At present we would only observe, that upon the Supposition the votes of the Chh do give us ground to Conclude, they Claimed Such a right, as we have Supposed they did; our observations, and reasonings which you Except against must we think, in the very letter of them, be vindicated, by the Common Sense of mankind - the principles we Supposed the Chh had by their votes Set up, appeard to us Ridiculous (If you will pardon the Expression) we attempted to Expose the nakedness, and absurdity of them. And tho’t our Selves Justifiable in doing So; & Should have Suspected you Sr, the least of any man, as finding faulty with us for it; after reading your declared opinion upon this point, in your Book Entitled misrepresentations Corrected &c for in the first page of your Preface you Say - I Desire that it may be Justly Considered by the reader, what is, and what is not Injurious and unhandsome treatment of an author, in a Controversy: And here I woud Crave leave to Say, that I Humbly concieve, a Distinction ought to be made, between opposing & Exposing a Cause, Or the arguments used to Defend it, and reproaching persons. he is a Weak writer, Indeed (you Say) who undertakes to Confute an opinion, but Dares not to Expose the nakedness & absurdity of it; nor the weakness And Inconsistence of the Methods taken and arguments used by any To maintain it, for fear he Should be guilty of Speaking Evil of those Things, and be Charged with reproaching them. If an antagonist is angry marginal At this, he thereby gives his readers too much occasion of Suspicion towards himself, as Chargable with weakness or bitterness now Sr have we done any thing more then to Expose the nakedness and absurdity of the principles, Set up by your votes, and to Shew, the weakness & Inconsistence of the methods taken, and arguments used, to maintain them and does this deserve the names of Taunting, Invidious Supercilious Language, you your Self being Judge. & for you Sr to be angry at Us for doing this (and we Cant th but think you was in Some little Degree of temper, when you wrote your message to us; If we adopt, And your own Sentiments and Expressions affords us to much occasion Of Suspicion towards you as Chargable with weakness or bitterness.

Nay Sr had we gone further then we have done, & taken full liberty in this respect, and represented this matter, So as that it might be vewed Justly in all its nakedness; yea Should we now Shew further, how the badness of [-] your Cause, as we apprehend; oblidges you to be Inconsistent, with your Self, Inconsistent with your own professed principles, and with things Conceded, and asserted, in the book above referred to; we Should be apt to think our selves Excusable, before you; Since, you have taught us to think, that this would be So far from Injurious treatment that Should we be afraid to do it, and to do it as thoroly as we Could . we must look upon our Selves to be foolish.

But we proceed now as we proposed, to Consider what you offer, to Convince us that the votes of the Chh, gave us no ground, to Conclude, that they Claimed a power to Determin, and Isue the matter of our Complaint themselves - you are pleased to Say, noting Can be more manifest, then that the Chh, in thus Expressing themselves Conditionally, avoid Saying or giving the the least Intimation, whether they think these things Should be finally Issued by themselves, or no - Answer. we never Disputed whether the Ch might not aim to wave any Positive Determination of that matter; for the time (viz) whether our Complaint Should be finally Issued by them, or no) and Indeed they Could Scarce do other wise, after they had voted to give us a hearing, before the Commissioners &c: but that they did avoid giving us the least Intimation, that we must abide their own Decision, If we rejected their proposal of referring to the Commissioners; we think far from being manifest at all; and this is all we Ever Suggested.

Here Sr let us Examine, whether the votes of the Chh do not manifestly marginal give us Such Intimations as these. we often find the Chh in these votes, Speaking of themselves as JUDGES of these things, and JUDGES to give JUDGMENT upon these things, and Speak of it as Dubious, whether the JUDGMENT of these things would be referred, to any OTHER JUDGES besides THEMSELVES. when we ought to lay our reasons, and produce our Evidences, to Support our Charge. that they are Such Judges, as have an undoubted right to Insist on this, and AUTHORITY to require it; and that whether it be Supposed to be proper that the Chh Should act as DECISIVE JUDGES in the affair or no: and this method of Stile Sr, you Seem to be fond of, for we find you Continuing it, in your late message; where you Say, as to our joining with you in referring the matter of your Complaints to other Judges besides the Chh . and tell us, that we must appear be[fore] the Chh, & Exhibit our Evidences and give the Chh opportunity to Interrogate &c before any Such Step Can be taken, as you propose: (that is) refering the matters of our grevance, to a Council (If it Ever be taken) Intim Intimating, that it was matter of great Doubt, and uncertainty, wh whether the Church would ever, give us that liberty; but it Seems that it is most manifest, that all this, Carries in it, not the least Intimation, that the Chh Claims a power to Determin and Issue the matter of our Complaints themselves. Suppose one Neighbour, or Brother, objects a Scandal, against another, and Desires of him Satisfaction, or Desires him to refer it &c: Should we hear hear upon it the person accused, upon it, thus Expressing himself: If the Judgment of these things be referred to any other Judges, besides my Self: and whether I yield that your objections, Should be Carried to the Chh, or a Council, or ANY OTHER JUDGES beside MYSELF or no . and that Such & Such things he has authority to require: whether it be Supposed, to be proper that he Should act as a Decisive Judge, in the affair or not. would it be most manifest, in Such a Case, that the accused avoided giving the least Intmations, of his Setting up his rights of Judging, and deciding the matter of the accusation? on the other hand, does not the Language Convey, Such Intimations in a very Strong manner, and give Just ground to Infer Such a Conclusion; and tho’ the thing thus Conditionally Expressed might Shew him at present undetermined Whether it were a point of prudence, or the most fit and propermarginal method of Issuing the matters of grievance, acting by HIMSELF acting as a DECISIVE JUDGE in the affair; yet would not his words manifestly Imply, that he has that Authority and may Improve that right If he pleases? and that it may be Expected he will do it, unless Some other measures be Come Into, to him more agreeable, and Should this unaccountable oddity be represented to him, in all its nakedness yea tho, it were done as thoroly, as the Complainants Could do it for his Conviction; Could he with propriety or Justice Charge him hereupon with Taunting language &c? and would not his being angry at it, Give to much occasion of Suspicion towards himself, as Chargable with weakness or bitterness? or would it be any Sufficient vindication of himself, Should he Say, that his giving the least Intimation of Such a thing Implyed Implied the most Glaring absurdity, and that nothing Can be more manifest then that he in Expressing himself thus thus Conditionally, avoided Saying, or giving the least Intimation, whether he tho’t the matter of Charge Should be finally Issued by himself or no - Such a Conditional way of Expressing himself would Indeed Seem to Intimate he was at present Some what unresolved whether to act as a Decisive Judge in the affair himself or whether he Should refer it to Some other Decision

as to what you Say under your 9th perticular, which Comes next under our Consideration, in order, we observe, that tho’ you Seem to offer at Some thing to Convince us, that we ought to lay before Church the reasons and Evidences by which we Expected to Support our Complaints; because you had Cited ,those of us who had absented from the Sacrament, before we had offered the reasons of our absenting &c: you Seem to lay great wait upon this, and by the time you Spend upon it, and the manner of your writing, to Conclude, that If all the rest Should fail, this must Stand the most Impartial Examination, and Secure the whole, you have Insisted on: and Indeed Sr our Eye was fixed upon it with no Small attention, but upon Examination, we found it utterly Insufficient, to afford the least measure, or Degree of Conviction; for be the Case as you Suppose; that you had a right to know what we had to offer in vindication of our Conduct, in absenting &c: we would Humbly ask: That when the Chh of Stockbridge, with their Revd Pastor, Camemarginal to be Informed, that the reason of our absenting, was matter of Scandalous offence; that lay with us against Pastor & Chh: that they had a right to Demand of us the reasons and Evidences, we had to Support the Charge Exhibited; unless they had a right to act as Judges: and the Case (notwithstanding, they were the party accused) properly lay before them, to Judge and act upon: and tho’ you pretend to Claim a right to judging; yet as you have never offered us the least Shew of reason to Support Such a right; as we are Humbly of the opinion, it is Impossible for you to do it, it being not only repugnant to reason, but Contrary to the universal Sentiment of mankind: we beg leave to Conclude, (that had we not Exhibited the reasons of our absenting &c; previous to your voting, and Sending unto us a Citation of appear before the Chh; to lay before them the reasons and Evidences of the Charge Exhibited &c:) we Can not be judged Criminal in refusing but we would further observe; the great mistake you have fallen into, in representing as putting in our Complaint, to Bar the Citation of the Church. when it is Evident, that our Complaint was laid before the Chh, previous to the Chhs voting, or Citing us to give the reasons of our withdrawing from the Sacraments, as will appear by Examining the Dates the respective papers bear; our Complaint bearing Date August 3d 1753; and your Citation So late as Septr 8th 1753.

2: the letter of your votes & message: which is as follows - This is to Inform you, that it was voted by the Chh at their meeting the last Tuesday that they Desire and Expect that you Should appear before the Chh the next Tuesday at my house &c: to lay before the Chh the reasons and Evidences by which you Justifie, the Charges you have lately publickly Exhibited, against the Chh and more Especially against the Pastor, and Some of the members; & also that the Chh then Expects, to hear, the reasons why you have lately absented &c. So that the very nature, and letter of your votes, & messages Shews, the falshood of the foundation of all your argument upon this head; we must therefore beg leave again to Conclude; that all the undoubted RIGHT the Chh had to Insist upon our laying, the reasons and Evidences of our Charge before them; and their authority to Demand it; built upon a false foundation, must with its Support, fall to the ground - as to what you Say under the 3d head we are not able to Comprehend the Inconsistency you Charge us with; for we Can’t See how we Contradict our Selves in asking, whether it was Honest to object the peculiar State of the Chh, as a reason you Could not join with us in Calling a Council, when it was Evident this was no objection to the Chhs acting in the other Case mentioned, we therefore Determined that we had reason to Conclude, that the objection against marginal Joining &c: founded on the peculiar State of the Chh was really no objection bec because the Chh paid no regard to it themselves; nor is our saying, we think it Evident, by the votes of the Chh that they Claim a power of Determining and Issuing the matters of Complaint themselves, at all Inconsistent with our asking, whether it be honest, to make Such an objection; for If this be the Case [-] the objection must We apprehend be y [-] and your Claiming this power, would be utterly Inconsistent with your making the objection, but as you are fond of fastening Contradictions upon us; you proceed to tell us under the 4th perticular that our Conduct, is not Consistent with our pretences - we have no Suspicions, that in our Conduct that we have Contradicted what we pretended; tho’ we are very Sensible, we have Contradicted what you have pretended, was our Duty (viz) to lay, the reasons and Evidences of our Complaints before the Chh, in which you Seem to Suppose the Inconsistence of our Conduct with our pretences lies; but we would Seriously ask? whether we Ever pretended, to lay our maters before you as an Ecclesiastical Judicature? whether we Ever pretended, to Submit them Even to the hearing of the Chh in that Shape? you Can’t but be Sensible, that we utterly Disclaimed this, and all ways Supposed the Church had no right, to act and Judge upon them in that Capacity . it is therefore Surprising to us, that you Should Suggest as you do under the 5th perticular; that our practice is Inconsistent, with our professed Desire to have the matters of offence removed: Can’t we heartily Desire thus? unless we Submit to lay the matters of Complaint before the Chh as Judges must we Concede that the Pastor and Chh are have a right to judge and determin in whether they are Innocent or guilty of the Charges we have Exhibited against them or fall unde the Imputation of Hypocrisy in professing a desire to have the matters of offence removed Can their be no other way of making our Desire manifest, but the method you Seemed to point the reasons and Evidences of our Charge before you, in the Shape you Contend for? must we concede? that the Pastor & Chh. have a right to require us as Judges (which is the Character you So Zealously Contend for) to have the Evidences Exhibited and give to Interrogate us upon them? or fall under the Imputation of Hypocrisy, in Professing a desire to have the matters of offence removed: we apprehend your reasoning under this perticular would humbly propose, whether your reasoning under this perticular, is not forreign to the points you have Set up (viz) your acting as Judges having power to Call us to an account, having a right to Demand of us, the reasons and Evidences of our Complaints; to Interrogate &c: & whether you were not Extreamly Streightned, for arguments & finally, whether you were not marginal Extreamly Confused - what you observe to us under your 7th perticular, we are Humbly of the opinion, Serves rather to fill up the Number of perticulars, then to add force to your reasons, against Complying with our Desire, of a Council &c. for is it not Evident the persons perticularly mentioned in the Complaint, are Included, in the Greater part of your Chh, we Say we have Special reason to be offended with, for making & publishing & afterwards Exhibiting, to the General Court a libellous Writing, Containing many, & various falshoods &c. why may we not then reasonably Expect, that ye their Case, So far as they have been Concerned in the affair of the libellous Writing, be Carried to a Council? what greater reason have to Excuse themselves, that the other members of the Chh, Can they do it? because we apprehend, they are more Especially Criminal in two things relating to that affair (viz) mentioned in they being as we apprehend d principle agents, in Contriving, promoting making & publishing Said libellous Writing.

2ly: that they, or Some by their procurement & privily, did forge & Counterfit the hand of Jona Devereux, by putting his name to the Said libellous writing with out his Consent or privily: ought they to be Excused, because they are Charged with more bad actions in the affair then Some of the other members? the reason Certainly Seems to be on other side or Can it be Supposed that the Chh have a right to judge and act upon those bad actions Certainly, If it be reasonable for us to Expect, that that Case, in which we apprehend the greater part of the Chh are Concerned, be Carried Immediately to a Council; it Cannot be unreasonable for us to Expect, that that those things mentioned, which, If true, rendered the Conduct of Some of the members much more Criminal, in that affair, be also laid before the Same Judges. and we would humbly offer, whether it is not reasonable to Expect, that Since you have Solemnly taken those brethren as you say under your Care, that in stead of doing anything to Screen them from a fair tryal, before proper Judges, that you Set before them, the reasonableness of Submitting the matter of our Complaints, in its Circumstances to a Council - And upon the whole Sr, as it manifestly appears to us, the Chh have no business, to set as Judges upon this our Complaint, we are under no obligation to appear before them, to lay you reasons &c by which we Expect to Support it, this Shall be done, in proper Season when the Case Comes to be laid before proper Judges - we are willing we We Trust, to do what is proper or becoming us to do, as Christians, wishing marginal the prosperity & peace of the Chh in Stockbridge. but as we have Exhibited our Complaint against the Pastor & Chh; we Esteem it their Duty, to Come to us, & Seek to give us Satisfaction, and not Cite and Require us authoritively, to Come and lay before them our reasons &c. and If upon their Coming they fail to give us Satisfaction, that they Consent and agree with us, to Refer the Consideration of our Greivance to an Ecclesiastical Council, mutually Chosen, who may hear and Decide the matter for us: which being agreed upon, we Stand ready to give them a Copy of all the charges particularly, we shall Exhibit against them, & Endeavour to Support before the Council. and thus we Shall be ready to do at any Seasonable time before their Session; that So Pastor & Chh may have opportunity, to Examine them and prepare to vindicate - we are we Say free to do this and as we Can’t but think this the most fit and proper method to be taken, so we Judge the most likely, to Settle the Difficulties Subsisting, and bring both you and us to a right understanding of things; but as to our appearing before the Chh, as a Judicature in this affair, we utterly Refuse it: nor will our meeting them as a body, Especially at first, Ever (as we think) be likely to Issue the affair; we shall be in Danger of provoking Each others tempers and advantage may be taken of words dropt in that Heat of Spirits; and So the breach widened rather then healed: as our writings are resented by you we have Some reason to fear that our words will be much more: words are liable to be missrepresented, and mistakes upon this head are too Common, as we have Experienced Since this Controversy began: but writings are not Exposed to this Hazzard, they will Speak for them selves the Same Language at all times - We pray you Sr to lay this our message before the Chh, that they may have opportunity, Either to Close with or Reject our proposal within mentioned & offered. & you will oblidge your Aggrieved Brethren & Friends -
Stockbridge April 6:th 1754
