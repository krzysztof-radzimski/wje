B73. Letter from Thomas Gillespie, September 19, 1748 B073.T.Gillespie-JE 9-19-48 edited Thomas Gillespie

B073. Thomas Gillespie to JE, 9/19/48. ANTS, f. 1748, #1. Printed in Edinburgh Quarterly Magazine 1 (1798), with alterations.

Letter to Mr Edwards 19th Septemr 1748

R V D Sir

I had the Favour of yours in Spring last for Which I heartily Thank You. I did not Want Inclination to make you a return long Ago as I prize your Correspondence but Some Things concurr’d that effectually Prevented me And that has given me Concern.

It was Desire to be Inform’d & Inclination to make You Understood how Some Passages in your Book on Religious Affections did Appear to me & Some other Your Real Friends & Well Wishers in this Countrey that Determind me to Presume to Offer you Some Free Remarks on the Mentiond Passagges in my late Letter & desire of further Information engages me now with all respect to make Some Observations upon Some Things in your Letter & I hope you will Pardon my Freedom & bear with me in it & Set me right Wherin you may find me to Misapprehend your Meaning or to Mistake in Another Respect.

You say "there is a great deal of Difference between these two Things Viz its being a Mans Duty that is Without Spiritual Light or Sight [--] to Believe, & its being his Duty to Believe Without Spiritual Light or Sight" Now D Sir the Difference here I am not Able to Conceive for All are bound to Believe the Divine Testimony & Trust in Christ which you Acknowledge & the Want of Spiritual Light or Sight does not loose from the Obligation one is laid Under by the Divine Command to Believe Instantly and at all Seasons the Promise as his Circumstances require Nor does it Excuse him in any Degree for not Believing. I own a Person Who has No Spiritual Light or Sight cannot eventually if by Light or Sight is Meant the Influence & Grace of the Spirit by Which ones Mind is Irradiated to take up the Object & Grounds of Faith so as to be Made Act that Grace Yet Still even When One Wants this it is his Duty and he is bound to Believe for we know it is a Maxim Ability is not Rule of Duty I Also Acknowledge that no Person Who is & Always has been Without Spiritual Light or Sight is bound nor is it his Duty to Believe he has Actually Believed or to Conclude he is really a partaker of the faith of Gods Elect.1 I have some Apprehension this is all you Meant by the Expressions I have Noticed & the Reasoning in Consequence of it or Certainly different Ideas are affix’d to Words With You & Among us.

There is indeed a great deal of Difference twixt its being ones Duty to Believe or to Act Faith & its being his Duty to Believe he has Believed or has Acted Divine Faith that is You Say You Apply Without Spiritual Light or Sight [--] to the Act of Believing by Which I Suppose you Intend none do really Believe Without Spiritual Light or Sight in Which I entirely Agree with You Tho the Word Duty is Ordinarily Supposd to Signify the Obligation the Person is under by the Divine Authority to Believe as Applied to the Matter of Faith And not [-] the Act of Faith put forth in Consequence of Such Obligation & had I not Suppos’d You plainly intended by the Expressions I Quoted from the Book the Duty or Obligation to Believe & Not An Act of Faith exerted I Should have made no Remark upon them. tis indeed Absurd for one to Conclude he has really Believd Without Spiritual Light or Sight or to Say One should Believe he has Believed eventually Without "these Things that Are Essentially Implied in Faith" but I must [---] ‘tis very proper to Say tis a Mans Duty to Believe Without Faith that is to put forth an Act of Faith on the Saviour however Void of Spiritual Light or Sight for if this was not the Truth the finally Impenitent Sinner could not be Condemnd for his Unbelief as the holy Ghost declares he Will be Jo 3,19. 8,24. & that Notwithstanding the Power of a Spirit of Faith must make him Believe.

You remark that I "Seem to Suppose that a Persons doubting of his own Good Estate is proper Opposite of Faith" & I own as it is a Believers Duty to expect Salvation through Christ Which in other Words is to Believe his Good Estate Acts 15,11. Gal 2,20. Eph 2,4 "doubting of it must be his Sin an Effect of Unbelief a Part of it & thus the proper Opposite of Faith Considerd in its full Compass & Latitude [-] doubting of [-] Good Estate by a True Believer & Unbelief in One Branch of it or one Part & Manner of its Acting are the Same Thing. [--] that Confident of ones Good Estate upon [--] Grounds & Faith & Conditions of it must be [---] Faith & Unbelief Are Opposed in Scripture marginal What is the Opposite of One Ingredient in Unbelief must be Faith in One Part of it one Thing that belongs to its Exercise A Persons Believing the Lord Will never leave nor forsake him Who is in a Gracious State Heb 13,5 is ownd to be his Indispensable Duty & this Comprehends or Supposes his being Confident of his Good Estate & is properly Divine Faith because it has the Divine Testimony now Cited on Which it Bottoms Jer 3,19. The Lord Says - thou Shalt Call me my Father & Shalt not turn away from me Which is evidently Faith Manifestly Belief of ones Good Estate or being Confident of it because the Expression must denote the Continued Exercise of Faith Crying Abba Father Ro 8,15. is Faith in the Lord as ones Father Which must have a being Confident of ones Good Estate Inseperable from it or rather Enwrapped in it [----] I Suppose What I have Mentiond is very Consistent with What you Say "That Faith & Persons Believing that they have Faith are Not the Same" for Ones Believing that he has Faith simply & by itself has for its Object the Mans inward Frame or the Actings & Exercises of his Spirit & Not a Divine Testimony but as I have laid the Matter A being Confident of ones Good Estate has for its Foundation the Word of God Heb 13,5 &c. Ultimately to be Sure, thus is One Way in Which Faith is Acted or One Thing in its Exercise.

I am far from Thinking [-] Unbelief or being Without Faith & doubting Whether they have Faith to be the Same Thing in an Unconverted Sinner Whom Your Words being Without Faith must Mean & therin we Entirely Agree. But I Must Think as the Believer his Doubting [--] he has Faith is Sinful because it is Belying the Holy Ghost denying his Work in him so [-] no Sin to Which that Doubting can so Properly be reducd As Unbelief. You know Dr Sir Doubting & Believing are Opposed in Scripture Mat 14,31. [-] 21,21. Mar 11,23. & I Cannot Exclude from the Idea of Doubting Questioning the Truth & Reality of a Work of Grace on Ones Soul for the Holy Ghost requires us to Believe the Reality of his Work in us in All its Parts Just as it is & Never Would Allow us, much less call us to Sin Believe a Falshood that one is Void of Grace When he has it that Good Might come of it the Person Might be Awaken’d from Security &c. I Jo 3,3. every Man that hath this Hope in him Purifieth himself as he is Pure Which I think Intimates in Proportion to One’s Hope [-] the Lord is his Father Will be his his Aim after Sanctification & his Attainment of it if so to Renouncemarginal this Hope throw it up at any Season on any Account Must be Unlawful Whence I Infer for the Believer to Doubt of his Gracious State to Call it in Question on Any Account Whatever so as to Raze it it is Simply Sinful 1 Jo 2,12,15. [--] I Write Unto You little Children because your Sins are forgiven You V 15 Love Not the World -- here Forgiveness of Sin is Us’d as a Motive & Incitment not to Love the World & this Reasoning of the Apostle Would lose All its Force for so long Was it Incumbent on a Believer at Some Seasons to think he was not within the Bond of the New Covenant thus he is bound ever to hold that Conclusion fix’d The Exhortation not to Cast Away ones Confidence certainly Comprehends a Call to Persevere in Believing Interest in the Lord & to Practise it att all Seasons Heb 10,35. Jobs Friends endeavour’d to Make him Question that the Root of the Matter was in him & to Conclude that he was an Hypocrite He resolv’d tho’ the Lord should slay him he Would Trust in him Ch 13,15. be Confident of his own Good Estate & Ch 27 3,5 All the While my Breath is in me - & V 5 ‘till I Die I Will not Remove my Integrity from me & We See from the Whole Tenor of his Book What there he resolv’d he Actually did Practise Never entertaind the Thought of Supposing the Lord was not his God Notwithstanding the Grievous Eruptions of Corruption in him in Quarrelling Sovereignty &c And in the End the Lord Condemn’d his Friends for Speaking of him the Thing that was not right pronounc’d Job his Servant Who had Said of him the Thing that is Right from Which tis to be Presumd he Was Approv’d in Guarding against Razing his State 2 Cor 1,12 What the Apostle Terms there his Rejoicing Was What Supposd his being confident of his Good Estate that he was participant of a Principle of Grace Which Made him Capable of Acting as he did with Godly Sincerity All Which with other Considerations do Satisfy me that a Believer never should raze his State on Any Account Whatever & that as has been Mentiond doubting of his Gracious State is Sinful one way of Unbelief its Acting in him tho’ not the Direct & Immediate Opposite of that Acting of Faith by Which a Person renounces his own Righteousness & Closes with Christ yet the Opposite of the Posterior Exercise of Faith in him in Certain Respects. Your Book is now Lent & therefore I Cannot take Notice as you Wish & I incline of What you Say on this Head P 76, 77. More Particularly than I have done However I have I think touch’d the Precise Point in Difference twixt us

You Observe I Seem to Intimate "A Persons being Confident of his own Good Estate is the Way to be deliverd from Darkness Deadness Backsliding & Prevailing Iniquity" And You Add that " you think Whoever Supposes this to be Gods Method of delivering his Saints When Sunk into an Evil Careless, Carnall & Unchristian Frame first to Assure them of their Good Estate & his Favour -- And so to Make that the Means of their Deliverance does Surely Mistake Gods Method of dealing with such Persons" here I think You represent the Case Strong for the Words in my Letter to Which you referr were "I have heard it Taught that the Believer was bound to Trust in the Lord in the Very Worst Frame he could be in & that Exercise of Faith was the Way to be deliver’d from Darkness Deadness Backsliding &c." And Afterwards I Said When Questioning Whether the Believer should ever Doubt of his State on Any Account Whatever "I know the Opposite has been Prescribd when the Saint is Plungd in the Mire of Prevailing Iniquity" Now as a Believer may be thus Plung’d & Yet Sin at that Instant his Grief & Burden Ro 7,24. & he May have the Hope & Expectation of being Relieved from it even then Ps 65,3. I do Not think my Words conveigh the Idea you Affix to them Also you Will Observe I do not Say that a Persons being Confident of his own Good Estate is the Way to be Deliverd from &c." but that the Believer Was bound to Trust the Lord in the Worst Frame &c. this I Mention precisely to State my Words & they are I think Very Defensible for the Believer is Calld to Trust in the Lord forever Is 26,4. if so When in the Situation Mentiond the Woman with the Issue of Blood her Touching Christ the Success is I Suppose A Call & Encouragment to Touch him by Faith for having the Worst Soul Maladies Heald Mar 5,25. Trusting the Lord for Needful Blessings in the Situation Mentiond gives him the Glory of his Faithfulness & Engages him to Act in the Believers behalf thus to do it is both Duty & Interest Jonah in a Course of Grievous Rebellion & Awful Chastisment for it When perhaps he had Actually disclaimd Interest in the Lord or Was in Danger of it Said He Would look again toward [--] Holy Temple" Ch 2,4. & V 7 When my Soul fainted Within me I remembred the the Lord & my Prayer came in Unto Thee into Thine Holy Temple. Here is marginal My Assertion Exemplified in Practice by a Believer I may Venture to Say in an Evil Frame [--] tho a Prophet he deliberatly Disobeyd the express Instructions of his great Lord in a Careless Frame for he sleepd Securely in the Sides of the Ship While an Awful Storm Rag’d raisd for his Sake & the Heathen Mariners every one Calld upon his God Ch 1, 5,6. So far was from Dreading as he had Reason the Lord Would Plead a Controversy With him for the Part he Acted Dismal Security Awful Carelessness in a Carnal Frame for he Declar’d to the Lord he Said to him in his Countrey that he Would Repent of the Evil he had Said he Would do to the Ninevites if they Turnd from their Evil Way And Assignd that for the Reason Why he fled to Tarshish2 & Displeasd him Exceedingly & he Was Very Angry that the Lord did not to to that People what he Threatend tho they turnd from Evil & upon this beggd the Lord Would take Away his Life & Said it was better for him to Die than to Live Ch 3,10. Ch 4 V 1-4. Horrid Carnality this for he car’d not What became of the Lords Honour if his Supposd One was Securd Was Sorry for the Success of his Mission Would rather have the Whole City Destroyd one of the most Populous the Sun Shone upon than he should Come under the Imputation of a False Prophet for Which there was yet no Foundation & as this was horrid Dreadful Selfishness may it in that View be termd Carnality Astonishing Pride thus Filthiness of the Spirit Worse than that of the Flesh in an Unchristian Frame for he sinn’d Presumptuously in the highest Degrees we may Suppose in Which ‘tis Possible for a true Believer so to Act & All Circumstances in his then Conduct evidence Such was his Frame Notwithstanding it Appears Under the Influence of the Spirit the Happy Turn began in him receiving Faith in the Lord as his God & being Confident of his Good Estate on Which he Prayed Was heard by his God V 7 & Deliverd out of his then Dismal and Dangerous Circumstances So that I have done more than I Was bound prov’d the Point not only in the Manner I Expressd it but in the Strong Light your Words a Comment on Mine had Set it for One Plain Scripture Instance Such as is that I have given is Sufficient to Prove Any Thing. And to All this it Cannot be excepted What I Mention was after Jonah had Acted as in Ch 2 V 4,7. for I might Detach What is Mentiond Ch 4, V 2, from the marginal Unchristian Also What we have Ch 2 V 4,7. Was his begun recovery What we have Ch 3 V 10 Ch 4, 1,3. a Relapse after Said recovery then in Consequence of Restoration to a better Temper he had received a New Prophetical Commission to go to Nineveh & Which he Executed & What was the Mean in Heavens Hand of his recovery in One Case must by Just Rules of Interpretation be Presumd to have been so in the other or to have been Suitable for that End & thus Usd by the Lord in his Sovereign Dispensation for that Purpose Which Comes to the Same. to Say that it is "the Lords method of Delivering his Saints When in a Backsliding Frame first to Assure them of their Good Estate & his Favour & so to Make that the Means of their Deliverance" is so far from being a Mistaking Gods Method as you Suggest that I give you the Words of the Holy Ghost as express & full for & as any Thing Possibly can by Jer 3, 12, 13 [-] 14. Turn O Backsliding Children Saith the Lord for I am Married unto You -- this Was to be Sure the Lords Intimating the Covenant Relation in Which he Stood to the Spiritual Israel Among them And V 22 of that Chapter the Lord Says return ye Backsliding Children & I Will heal your Backslidings & in the Close of the Verse we have the Lords thus Assuring them of their Good Estate & his Favour the effectual Mean of their Backsliding being heald -- behold we Come Unto thee for thou art the Lord our God. Hos 14,1. O Israel return unto the Lord thy God for thou hast fallen by Thine Iniquity here the first Words of the Lords Message to his Spiritual Israel are that the Lord was their God & the Expression fallen by Iniquity Conveighs a Very Strong Idea perhaps as Strong, as is Comprehended in your Words I think Were a Comment on Mine Evil &c Frame & I Must think this Verse is So Express’d to Work on Holy Ingenuity in them for its Revival When Under the Ashes of Corruption it Would perhaps be no Difficult Matter to Multiply Scripture Testimonies of such kind but these Adduced Are I think full Proof of the Point for Confirmation of Which they are brought tis like the Love of Christ Constrains the Believer to return from Folly as Well as to other Things in other Respects 2 Cor 5,14. I Might Argue here from the efficacy of the Love of God Apprehended the Genius of the New Creature & Nature in in Believers & a Variety of other Topicks but Choose Without marginal Expatiating to Confine my Self to Precise Scripture Testimonies. As to What you Say "that of All the Multitudes you have had Opportunity to Observe you Never knew one Dealt with in this Manner [-] but have known Many brought back from great Declensions that Appeared to be True Saints but it was in a very Diverse Way from this first - Conscience Awakned - they brought into great Fear of the Wrath of God - his Favour hid - they Subjects of a kind of New Work of Humiliation brought to great Sense of Deserving Gods Wrath - While they yet Feard it before God has deliverd from Apprehension of it & Comforted with a renewd Sense of his Favour" All I Observe upon this is the Way I have laid down Obviously is that the Lord declares in his Word he takes for bringing back his People from Declensions & thus in it that Mercy is to expected Persons dont perceive every Thing that Passes Within them far less are they capable to give a full distinct Account of every Thing of Such kind - Experiences of Christians are to be brought to the Touchstone of the Infallible Bar & to Stand or fall by it the Bible is not to be brought to their Test & Judgd of by them I own We May Mistake the Sense of Scripture but it is so Obvious in the Passages I have Just Quoted that I Cannot see how it can be Misapprehended.

I cannot say any Thing now About other Remarks I Made on your Book because I have it not to look into I Understand your Mind better by What you have Wrote & if Any Difficulty Shal remain after Comparing your Book & Letter I May come to Propose it to You afterwards

What You Wrote About the Case of Temptation was Very Agreable & I thank you for it. Shal Now State the Case more Plainly because I Want much your further Thoughts upon it ‘tis Precisely this A Person finds himself beset by Evil Angels What if I remember Right Voetius Terms Obsessio & One in that Situation Obsessus they Incessantly break into his Body & Mind Somtimes by Vain at other Seasons by Vile Thoughts Now by the Thoughts of a Business Neglected A Thing Seasonable to be done then by a Scripture Text or an Engaging Thought of Some Spiritual Truth When entrance is not to be had Another Way & by a Variety of other Methods. do All they can Perpetually to Letter3 to Mr Edwards Pag 6 Line 13 Tarshish Ch 4,2. marginal Thus Would rather the Lord Should Want the Honour Would redound to his Name by the Repentance tho’ only outward of the Ninevites And the Whole City Should be Destroy’d one of the largest the Sun Shone upon & the most Populous himself shold lose the Honour & Comfort of being the Instrument of its Preservation than he Should fall under the Imputation of being a False Prophet for Which there Would yet have been no Foundation Horrid Carnality this for as it was Dreadful Selfishness it may in that View be termd Carnality Astonishing Pride thus Filthiness of the Spirit Worse than that of the Flesh And All Circumstances of his Conduct Considerd he was not only in an Unchristian Frame but an Inhuman One and he Sinnd Presumptuously in One of the highest Degrees we may Suppose in Which ‘tis Possible for a Believer so to Act

Pag 7 Lin 10 It is so far from being a Mistaking Gods Method of dealing with Such Persons as You Suggest (Pardon me D Sir) to Say that it is "The Lords Method of delivering his Saints When in a Backsliding &c Condition first to Assure them of their Good Estate And his Favour and so to make that the Means of their Deliverrance" that I give You the Words of the Holy Ghost for it as Express and full as Any Thing Possibly can be. Pag 8 Line 3 Lin to Teize defile & Discourage he is Conscious of the Whole Transaction & finds his Spirit broke by it &c. goes not About to Reason with Satan knows the Inexpediency of this Course is aware Satan wants no better than that he Pray Much & long Against his Temptations & so Won’t Pray himself out of Breath by his Instigation is Convinc’d the Remedy is to get them kept out of Body & Mind Trus[t] in Dependance on the Lord Use of Mechanical or Medical Moral Religious Means for that End because Experience Shews All of them are Expedient Advantageous in their Place but all is in vain no relief for him Relish of Divine Things Wore off the Mind No Comfort it renderd Callous by Cruel Constant Buffetings he Cries but the Lord hears not by What I Understand this is a Just Representation of the Case And Will lead You to the Knowledge of other Circumstances in in it What Would you Advise Such a Person to do how Shal he Recover a Savour of Spiritual Truths And Objects.

I Wonderd you Said Nothing in your Letter about What I Mentiond in Mine respecting Supposd Immediate Revelations of Facts & Future Events as Special Favours Conferrd on Some Special Favourites of Heaven. I give in to your Sentiments on that Nice Point expressd in the three Treatises you have publish’d And greatly like What Mr Brainerd said on this Subject as Mentiond I think by You in the Funeral Sermon on him Which I Perusd with a great deal of Pleasure And Shal Now Mention Some Things Said in Favour of that Principle of Which People are Very Tenacious. That I May have your Answers to them Which Will be a Singular Favour done me for Certain Reasons And Jo 16,13 is Affirmd to be an Express Promise of Such a Thing. tis Urg’d the Thing is Not Contrary to Scripture & therefore May be. tis Argued Jo 13,24--7 is an Example of it an Intimation what the Lord Will do in Such kind When it Pleaseth him till the End of Time. ‘tis Pretended and indeed this is the Strength of their Cause The Thing is a Simple Fact has Nothing to do With the Bible therfore Nothing About it is to be expected in Scripture & Simply to Deny it in All Cases is Daringly to Limit Sovereignty The Lord has Not Said he Will not Grant it and how dare Any Say it Cannot be tis Reasond there are Numbers of Well Attested Instances of the Thing in different Ages & Places Facts are Stubborn Things and to Deny them All is Shocking an Overturning All Moral Evidence. It is insisted on the Thing has been formerly tis Confessd and Why May it Not be Now. We are told a Considerable While before a Thing happend it has been Impressd on the Mind in All its Circumstances Which Exactly happend in every Point if When Askd What one can Say to this he Says perhaps it Was from Satan to this tis answerd does he know Future Contingent Events The Reply is at Hand tis not above him to Figure a Thing on the Fancy long before Which he is resolved by Some Means to bring About but to All this ‘tis Answerd by Advocates for Immediate Revelations Such Reasoning Tends to Sapp one of Main pillar of Evidence of Divinity of the Scriptures Prophecies I have by What I remember given you the Force of the Argument to Establish What What has had I too Well know very bad Effects as Commonly Mannagd in Britain as well as in New England a History of Instances of them Would not Want its Use & Materials for it are not Wanting I Will long Much to See What You Say in Way of Reply to All this. I am Sure you Can’t employ Time better than in framing it. I Should have Mentiond the Authorities of Eminent Divines are brought to bear on them Whose Stomachs Stand at Swallowing down Things like Additions to the Bible Fleming in the Fulfilling of the Scriptures Dr Goodwin &c.4 but on this it has been Pleasantly Observd the Authority of the Worthies in the Eleventh of the Hebrews Would have done a Good deal better. I have Some Apprehension this is the Point the Truth in Which the Lord is to Clear up in this Age

I Read Your Humble Attempt &c with much Satisfaction Was Charmd with the Scriptures of the latter Day Glory Sett in one Point of Light Do think Humbly Your Observations on Lowman5 have great Strength of Reason. The Killing of the Witnesses as Yet to Come has been to me a Grievous Temptation for Which Reason I perusd with Peculiar Pleasure What you Say on that Subject But if you Answer the Objection it Would Appear the Seventh Trumpet is to Sound soon after the Resurrection of the Witnesses & the Kingdoms of the World &c. but that has Not happened therfore the Witnesses are Not killd if this You Answer I have forgot I Should have Mentiond it Seems Evident the Doctrine of Immediate Revelations must be Simply Denied as Unscriptural & thus Allowd in No Case or must be Allowd in its full Compass and Latitude let the Consequences of it be What they Will for if the Thing is Allowd Possible Reasonings About its Effects Will Not Conclude nor Avail I can See No Mids twixt the two Things. That Principle taken for Granted by Almost All in All Time Past is as I Mentiond in my last Letter to me a Surprizing Thing.

Mr Whitefield Arrivd at Edinburgh Wednesday [--] last & Was to Preach on Thursday Evening but as I am 15 Miles from the City of Which 2 Miles Sea I have not yet heard of the Effects of his Preaching or the Number of the Audiences I Wish they May be as frequent as When he was Last here May Divine Power Specially Attend his Ministrations We Need it Much as we are indeed Generally fallen Under great Deadness I Believe he Will find Use for All his Prudence & Patience in dealing With us for Different Reasons With great Pleasure Friends to Vital Religion & to him Are informd he is to Make No Collections at this Time. I Was glad to hear you Write he Labourd with Success in marginal New England in rectifying Mistakes he had favourd About Intimations Made by the Lord to his People &c. & Heartily Wish he may be Directed to Apply an Antidote here Where it is Also Needed

I have tird You with a long Epistle & Shal therfore now break off What You Was Pleasd to FavoUr me With Upon the Difficulty Started from Ro 8,28. was very Acceptable & I thank You Much for it I Will expect a Letter from You first Opportunity after this Comes to Hand And in it All the News of New England Particularly Some Account of the State of Religion with You It gives me Pleasure to Think I may Write You my Sentiments on every Thing Without Reserve Please take the Trouble to Make my Affectionate Compliments to my Friend Mr Abercrombie6 When You See him or Write to him & tell him I Remember I am in his Debt for a Letter I hope the Ship I am informed of for Carrying this is not saild & therfore twill not be so long in Coming to your Hand after being Writt as my last

Was
I Am
