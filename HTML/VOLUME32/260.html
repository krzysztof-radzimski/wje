A233. Letter to Joseph Bellamy, December 1, 1757 233.JE-J.Bellamy 12-1-57 Jonathan Edwards

A233. JE to Joseph Bellamy, 12/1/57. ANTS, f. 1756-59A, #6-7. Addressed on back "To the Rev. / Mr. Joseph Bellamy / at Bethlem."

Stockbridge decem. 1. 1757.

Rev. & dear sir,

Yesterday I received your two Letters of the 12 & 17 of

Novem. But I saw nor heard Nothing of Mr. Hill.1 -- I thank

you for your Concern that I may be useful in the World. --

-- I lately wrote you a Letter informing you of our Choice

of a Council to sit here on the 21 of this month; & inclosed

in it a Letter missive to Mr Brinsmade, who is one of the

Council. I hope before this Time you have received -- don't

fail of letting me see you here; for I never wanted to see you

more.

As to the Question you ask about the Christs Argument in

John 10:34, 36 35, 36. I observe First. That it is not [---] all

Princes of the Earth that are called God's in the Old Testament,

But only the Princes of Israel that ruled over Gods People. The Princes

that are called Gods in the Psalms 82 are, in the same Sentence

distinguished from the Princes of the Nations of the World. "I have

said ye are GODS: But ye shall die like Men, & fall like one of the

PRINCES.

Secondly, the Reason why those Princes of Israel were called Gods, was

that they prefig Rulers & Judges of Gods Israel were Figures of Him, who

is the true King of the Jews & Prince of Gods People, who is to rule

over the House of Jacob forever, the Prince & Saviour of Gods Church that

gathered from all Nations of the Earth; who is God indeed. The

Throne of Israel properly belonge who is God indeed. The Throne

of Israel properly belonged or of Gods People properly belonged to Christ;

He only is the proper Heir to that Crown: And therefore the Princes

of Israel are said to Sit upon the Throne of the Lord 1 Chronicles 29

23 and the Kingdom of Israel is called the Kingdom of the Lord 2 Chronicles.

13. 8. And because took the Throne as the Antitype[-] of those Kings,

therefore He is said to sit upon their Throne. Thus, the Princes of

Israel are called Gods, & s in that Psalms 82., and sons of God, or Chil

all of them Children of the most high, being appointed Types, & re-

markable Representations of the true son of God, & in him of the

true God. They were called Gods, & sons of God, in the same manner

as the Levitical sacrifices were called an Attonement for sin, and in

the same manner as the manna was called the Bread of Heaven &

Angels Food. These things represented, & by special des divine

designation, were Figures of the true Attonement, & the of Him who

truly was Angel's Food; And in the same sense as saul, the Person

especially pointed at in the Psalms 82, is called the Lords Anointed,

or Messiah or Christ, which are the same. And it is to be observed

that these Typical Gods are distinguished from the true God that &

was to come as their Antitype, in the next sentence, Psalms 82:8.

Arise, O GOD thou JUDGE of the Earth; For thou shalt inherit all na-

tions. This verse with the two foregoing in their Connection imparts

thus

thus much This is a wish for the coming of Christ. That King marginal

that should reign in Righteousness & judge uprightly; who was to in-

herit the Gentiles as well as the Jews. And the words as they Stand

in Connexion with the two preceding verses, impart thus much.

"As to you the temporal Princes & Judges of Israel, God has you are called

Gods & sons of God, being exalted to the Place or seat of Kings, Judges

& saviours of Go P God Gods People, the Kingdom & Heritage of Christ:

But you shall die like men, & fall like other Princes. whereby it ap-

pears that you are no Gods, nor any one of you the true Son of God,

B which your Injustice & Oppression also shews. But Oh that

He who is truly God, the true Judge & s & just Judge & savi-

our (who is to be King over Gentiles as well as Jews) would come

would come & Reign. --- It is to be Observed that when it is said

in this v. arise O GOD. The word GOD is Elohim. The same that

is used v. 6. I have said ye are God GODS. I have said ye are

Elohim.

_________________________

Thirdly. t as to those Words of X Christ John 10:35. If he called

them Gods UNTO WHOM THE WORD OF GOD By th I suppose

that by the word of God its coming to these Princes of Israel, is meant

their being set forth by special & express divine designation to be Types

or Figurative significations of God's Mind. Those things which God

had appointed to be Types to Signify Gods mind, were a visible Word.

Types are called the Word of the Lord; as in Zechariah 11:10-11. & Chap.

4. 4, 5, 6. The word of the Lord of L God came to the Princes of Israel

two way both as they were by the became subjects of a Typical Res

Representation of a divine Things; which was a visible word of God;

& also as this was done by express, divine designation, as they were mark'd

to this End by an express word audible & legible word: as in Exodus 22

28. & Psalms 82:1. Thus the Word of God came to Jacob as a Type

of Christ 1 Kings 18:31. And Elijah took twelve Stones according to the

Number of the Tribes of Jacob, UNTO WHOM THE WORD OF THE

LORD CAME, saying Israel shall be thy name. The word Israel

is PRINCE Of GOD. Jacob being by that Express, divine designation appointed

as a type of Christ the true prince of God, in his prevailing in his

wrestling with God to save himself from & his Family from destruction

by Esau, that was then coming against him, & obtaining the Blessing

for himself & his seed. Now

________________________________

Fourthly. Christs Argument lies in those words The scripture can-

not be broken. That Word of God by which they are called Gods,

as Types of him that is truly God, must be verified, which they

can't be unless the Antitype be truly God. For it was upon that

acc They are so called as Types of the Messiah. And as such

they are called or of the Anointed one (which is the same) or the

sanctified or Holy one. or him that was to be sent which were

all Known Names among the Jews for the Messiah (see

Daniel 9:24-25. Psalms 89:19-20. Psalms 16:10. John 9:7.2) But they

it was on this Account that the Types or Images of the Messiah

were called God's, because He that they represented was God indeed.

If he were not God, the word by which they were called Gods

could not be verified. As the word by which the Legal marginal

sacrifices were sa called an Attonement, and are said to

attone for sin, was true, in no other sense, than as they

had relation to Chris the sacrifice of Christ, a true

Attonement. If Christs sacrificed had not truly

atoned for sin, the word that called the Repre-

sentations of it an Attonement, could not be

verified. So if Jesus had not been the true

Bread from Heaven, & Angels Food indeed, the

scripture that Called the Type of him Bread of

from Heaven,& Angels Food, would ha not

have been verified, but would have been broken.

These sir, are my thoughts on John 10:34. &c--

I am your's most affectionately,

J. Edwards.

P.S. decem. 5.

Sir. The opportunity for Conveyance of my Letters to mi-

nisters Chosen to be of the Council your way, not

being very good. I here send other Letter's desiring

you to take the Care of conveying them with all

possible care & Speed.

Mr. Bellamy
