& so all that we have done will prove worse than in vain [-] for the Consequence will be the future prejudi establishing a Prejudice in the minds of the six Nations against any such attempts of the future & putting all designs of this nature more Effectually out of our Reach it may be for two or three Generations & according to the Genius of the People they will never Trust

[---] Yea I apprehend more danger in than this the stockbridge Indians are in such a Rustle & in so uneasy & future Passions by Reason of the state of the th by Reason of the the Pres. situation of things that th I think there is real danger that most of them that if the there be no alteration they will break up & last Col.

I expect he by Reason of our Those difficulties to be Represented by some of the Family of the W---ms's & those that are related to em to be represented as very unfit for the Place of I am in. & should not wonder A mischief maker &c & should not wonder if they should strive to Remove me However I will discharge in a good Consc. tis necessary here shou those whom have the Governmt of the state of things which they never can know but by information from some here. I have no Col. D. [---] notwithstanding all his past the forementioned Conduct Professes still to be my Hearty Friend Friend. that often men dont Know their own Hearts that the the one of those things I think is certainly true as viz. that he of his Est Affection are the same that they were him his He is greatly changes as to his Esteem or if He be my Cordial Friend as He says it Happens so strangely that Friendship in Him acts like the Enmity in other Persons --I have no Interest to make for myself or any of my Friends. It has of late been abundantly given [-] out that I am seeking the female school for some of my Family which is perfectly a groundless surmize. I have not the least inclination to any such Thing.

I entirely trust your Honours wisdom as the use you make of this Letter that you will make such an use of this Letter as to do good & not [any]9hurt with it. Laying you under no Restraint Lay no Limitation but such as shall be set by your own disgression.

However I will endeavour alwaies to discharge a good Consc. at the same Time endeavouring so far as may be consistent with this to live peaceably with all men. we are here in a remote & hidden Corner where a vast deal of publick moneys & moneys given for Charitable & pious Purposes have expended & I am satisfied that the O is in no measure sensible after what manner much of it has been spent. Tis absolutely necessary that those that are first in the ordering & Governmt of our affairs should know the state of things which they never Can know but by Information from some that live here which never would be given if fear of incurring the displeasure of particular Persons must alwaies be sufficient to prevent

---Col. d. notwithstand all that has been forementioned professes still to be my Hearty Friend and declares that what He has said & done has not been from disaffection but Judgmt to which I have nothing to say. but only that if He is not mistaken through ignorance of his own Heart (as many times Persons are) It strangely happens that Friendship in Him acts like Enmity in other Folks.

I shall prescribe to your Honour no Limitations in the Use you shall make of this Letter but what are set by your own discretion entirely trusting your wisdom to use it so as to do good & not hurt with it.

I am y sir

your most humble servt

& obliged servt

J. E.
