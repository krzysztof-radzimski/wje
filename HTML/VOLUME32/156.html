A136. Letter to Mary Pepperrell (Draft), November 28, 1751 136.JE-M.Pepperrell 11-28-51 Jonathan Edwards

A136. JE to Lady Mary Pepperrell, n.d. [11/28/51]. Draft. ANTS, f. 1751, #11.

Madam. when I the Last spring was at your House in Kittery among other Instances of your kind and Condescending Treatmt of me you was this that when I had some discourse with sir William Concerning the Indian affair & Stockbridge and He Generously offered his Kind offices me any assistance in the Business of my mission here that his Acquaintance & Correspondence in London enabled Him gave him Advantage for & to propose my writing to him our on our affairs you were pleased on this Occasion to in vite me to write to you at the same time. If I should fail neglect to do as the you then proposed I should not neglect to doing a Xtian Part to you But fail of doing my self a great honour. But as it wa I know from the small acquaint. I had with you [-] that a Letter of Compliments would not be agreable to one a Lady of your disposition & especially under your present melancholy Circumstances. so the writing of such a Letter is very far from my design Intention or Inclination. when I saw the Evidences of your great deep sorrow under the awful frowns of Heaven in the (then late) death of your one only son it made an Impression on my mind that is not yet worn out that turned my disposition to quite other things than Flattery & Ceremony. when when you mentioned my writing to you I soon determined what should be the subject of my Letter. It was that which appeared to me to be the most proper subject of Contemplation for one in your Circumstances and a subj the subject which was most like to be the above all others appeared to me to be the a proper & sufficient source of Consolation to one under your Heavy affliction and this was the L the L. Jesus X with Regard to two especially to two things viz. his Amiableness & Love The former or His infinite worthiness & that we should love Him & take him for our only Portion rest & Hope & Joy the other his great & unparalleled Love to us. And I have been of the same mind ever since being determined if G. favoured me with opportunity [-] to write to your Ladyship these Things should be the subject of my Letter ---I will now therefore G. favouring me [-] with opportuni begin with the former of these Let us think dear madam a Little of the Loveliness of the Re of our Blessedness & his worthiness & his worthiness that our whole souls should be swallowed up with Love to Him & delight in him & that marginal we should entirely [ ] call him [ ] to be [--] Rest in him have sweet Complacence & R satisfaction of soul in & salve our Hearts in his Excellency & Beauty whatever else we are deprived of. th. The SS. assures us of his divin abundantly of his proper divinity. so that we consider our Redeemer the him that that came into the O in our nature and died for us as truly truly possessed of all the Fulness of the di that Infinitely B Glory of the Godhead. we are told that He is the brightness of Gods Glory & the express Image of his Person. His Infinite Greatness & majesty. his Infinite wis.. his most perfect infinitely perfect holiness & purity Righteousness & Goodness [--] He is the brightness of The He is called the brightness of Gods Glory & the Express Image of his Person1 He is such a present Image that Image that in viewing which G. sees in the viewing of which G. the F. had all his Infinite Happiness from E.2 The Happiness Et. & immutable Happiness of the deity Hims. is Represented in SS. as a kind of social Happiness tis the society of the Persons of the Trinity. Prov. 8. 30. Then was I by Him as one brought up with Him & I was daily his delight rejoycing alwaies before Him This Glorious Person in the view of whose Infinite amiableness perpetual & Eternal view of whose Beauty G. the F. is infinitely Happy G. has G. sent into the O to be the Light of the O that by him the Beauty of the Deity might shine forth in Great fullness in the brightest & fullest manner to the Chil. of men that we might & we beheld the Glory of the deity with greatest Ad & Infinite wisd. has contrived that we should behold the Glory of the deity to the Greatest advan- d vantage in the Face of J. X to the greatest advantage & in such a manner as should be most adapted to the Capacity of poor fee worms & w so as should tend most to Engage & our attention to all encourage and allure ou & in our Hearts & give us the most full & perfect Acquiescence & delight. for X having taken the natu by his Incarnation having come down as it were from his Infinite height above us be having become one of us our Kinsman & Brother & his Glory shining through to us through his human nation nature the manifestation is marvellously adapted to ou Qualified to suit the nature of the human sight & his Glory is the Effulgence of his Glory is attempered to our sight. He is indeed a Person of Infinite majesty to fill our souls with the greatest Reverence & adoration But there is nothing in it that needs to terrify us for his Infinite majesty is Joined marginal with the most superlative meekness & humility. so that in the whole there is nothing terrifying or forbidding. There is in may be infin the utmost possible Reverence & hum blene Pre ab & b abasemt. attend [--] with the most boldness of most free the most free access the most Inti mate Intercourse & familiar Intercourse embrace & attended with sweet surprize when we view his Greatness & majesty we are Kept from fear & flight by the view of his Infinitel Gentleness & Humility. when we & when we view his Love marvellous Love & abasemt we we are kept from an indecent familiarity by the view of his infinite majesty. & by all together we are filled with sweet surprize most Reverential Love. humble boldness & Familiarity. delightful adoration & sweet surprise.

in Therein Glory of X that is properly & in the highest sense divine all the brightness of Glory that is the deity who is Light & inf a & Luminary infinitely bright. such is the exceeding brightness of this sun of Righ. that the Light brightness of the natural sun is as darkness in Comparison of it yea black as sackcloth of Hair. and therefore when he shall appear in his Glory the sun shall disappear as th brightness of the sun shall disappear as the brightness of the Little stars do when the sun rises. Isai 24. 23. Matt. 24. 29. Rev. 6. 12. But yet altho' ligh his Light he his beams are exceed Light is so bright & his Beams so exceeding strong yet & his Beams go forth with infinite strength yet as they flow app apear in the Love proceed from X in hi the Character of the Lamb of G. & shine through his meek & Lowly humanity they are infinitely gentle & mild not dazzling & painful to the fe our feeble Eyes [-] but most comforting vivifying & strengthening healing. Like a Gentle Eye salve Thus the sun of Right. arises on them that fear Gods name with healing in his wings i.e. in his Beams Mal. 4. 2. It is like the Light of the morning as a morning without Clouds as the dew on the Grass Causing the souls of his People under whose Influence the souls of his People are as the Tender Grass springing out of the Earth by Clear shining after Rain Thus are the Beams of his Beauty & brightness fitted for the for th support the healing & Ref Reviving of the afflicted He heals the broken in sp. & bindeth [-] up their wounds He comes down as the Rain on the mown Grass the spirits of his People that are as it were cut down by the scythe of adversity. like the [-] Rain on the mown Grass as as and as the showers that water the Earth Ps. 72. 6.

But especially is the Glory are the Beams of Xs Glory not only softened but infinitely sweetend by that other thing which I proposed to Consider. viz. his Love his dying Love un paralleld Love his dy dying Love And here many things are to be considered one is that the Glory of Xs Person very much consists in that infinite Grace that he Goodness & Grace which has so marvellous a manifestation in his Love to us . The Apostle John Tells us that G. is Light marginal3 1 Joh. 1. 5. & that He is Love 1 Joh. 4. 8. 16. and his Light is an Infinitely sweet Light because it is the Light of Love and especially appears so in the Person of th our Redeemer that who was infinitely the most wonderful Instance of Love the ver that ever was. All the excellencies of the G the Perfection All the Perfections of the deity have instance their brightest manifestation in the work of Redemption vastly more than in the work of Creation. And for the in other works we see Gods Back Parts but here shines the Glory of his Face 2 Cor. 3. 18. yea in this work is opend the Infinite treasures of Gods Heart. And this work is a work Eph. 3. 8, 9, 10.

But this work is a work of Love to us and a work that X is the Author of. His Loveliness & his Love bot have both their most marvellous their greatest manifestation in his dying for us those last sufferings he endured for us at his death. Therein appeared above all appear'd the Holiness of X in that his Love to the F his hatred of sin & his Love to the Father in the G. in that when He desired to save sinners rather than that a suitable testimony should not be born against sin it & rather he & He would submit that strict Justice should take place in condemning it & pu in its condemnation & punishmt in his own souls being pourd out unto death. and all Rom. 8. 3. And ra- ther than than G. the salva. of sinners should be in Just & such was his regard to Gods Honour that rather than the desired happiness of hims should int injure it He would give up hims. a sacrifice for sin. Thus his in the same act he appears as inf in the greatest conceivable manifesta. of his Infinite Hatred of sin & also infinite Grace & Love to sinners. His Holiness appeared like a fire burning with infinite vehemence against sin. at the same time that his Love appeared like a pure sweet flame burning with an infi nite fervency of Benevolence. Tis the Glory of his Love to us filthy sinners that tis an infinitely pure Love & it tends to endear his Holiness the sweetness and endearment of his Holiness that it has its greatest manifesta. in such an act of Love to us. All the vertues of X have their Greatest mani both divine & human have their greatest manifestation in hi that marvellous act of his Love [-] [-] his offering up hims. a sacrifice for us under those extreme sufferings. herein he appears his Infinite wisd. as herein he hath abounded towards us in the Riches of his grace in all wisd. of & prudence Eph. 1. 8 Herein appears most his strict Justice. herein above all other Things appeared his mea humili the humility of his Human nat Nature in being willing to descend so low for us herein th in h his behaviour under those last sufferings above all other things appeared his meekness Obedience to G. his submission to his disposing will his meekness [4]4 his Patience his meekness herein Appeared his meekness when He was as a Lamb to the slaughter. herein appeared his f and opend not his mouth but only in Prayer that G. would forgive his Crucifiers . & in hi & what an affecting manifestati & endearing a manifestation in [thing] is the manifesta. of his Excellency & amiableness when in it chiefly shines forth in such an act of Love to us.

The Love of X another way tends to sweeten & Endear all his vertues & excellencies viz. as his Love has brought him into such a Relation to us as that our Brother Elder Friend our Elder Brother our Head our H our Lord our Head & spiritual Husband & hath brought us into so strict an union with him that we are the mem are our souls are his beloved Bride yea, we are the members of his Body his Flesh & his Bone Eph. 5. 30.

Now Madam Let it be Considered Let us Considered what suitable Provision G. has made for our Consolation under all our afflictions in giving us such a saviour a Redeemer of such Glory & such Love especially when it is Considered what were the Ends of that Great manifestation of his L Beauty & Love in his death. He suffered that we might be delivered he and His soul was exceeding sorrowful even unto death that we might have the Light of Life to take away the sting of sorrow and that we might have everlasting Consolation He was opp oppressed & afflicted that we might be supported. he was overwhelmed in the darkness of death & of Hell that we might have the Light of Life He was cast into the furnace of Gods wrath that we might swim in his the Rivers of Pleasure. his heart with was overwhelmed in a flood of sorrow & anguish that our Hearts might be filled with and overwhelmed with a flood of Et. Joy.

And now let it be Considered what Circumstances our Redeemer now is in. He was dead but [-] is alive & he lives forever more death may [-] deprive of dear friends but it cant deprive us of this our best Friend. And we marginal have this Friend this mighty Redeemer to go to under all affliction one who can who is not one that cant be touched with the feeling of our affliction. He having suffered far Greater sorrows than ever we have done & if we are vitally united to him the union can never be broken. & c & b & his Love will never fail. Theref. an it will remain when we die & when heaven & Earth are dissolved . therefore we need in this we may be Confident. we need not fear tho the Earth be Removed. in Him we may triumph with everlasting [-] Joy. our souls m. so when now when we are weary now when storms & tempests arise we may have Resort to him who is is an hiding Place to th from the wind & a Covert from the Tempest when we are thirsty we may come to him who is as Rivers of W. in a dry Place. when we are weary we may go to him who is as a the shadow of a great Rock in a weary Land. & w having found him who is as the Apple Tree among the Trees of the wood we may sit under his shadow with great delight & his Fruit may be sweet to our Taste.
