Jesus the mediatour of the new covenant. -

VII. Particular. 1740

In how by this point I now 1.How a Cov. be-

tween G. & fallen Creatures 2. What Cov. that is then

X is Spoken of in the text 3. Why it is called the

new Cov. 4. How Jesus X is the mediator of that

new Cov. 5. How Xtians are come to the medi-

atour.

& here this

I. Thing is that a Cov. between G. and fallen Creatures

required a mediatour. It was not meet that G. should

have to do with fallen man ..... in way

of Cov. unless it was by a mediator.

a Cov. is a kindly invention or a least it is

ever so in G. towards his Creatures.

it is a manifestation of Goodness and won-

derfull Condescension in G. towards Creatures

that he that is infinitely above em should should

deal with them in such a way & become obliged

to Creatures .... as one in Cov.

with them who might deal with them only in

a way of over sovreignty.

But it was not meet that an offended

G. should this deal with a Rebel in

such a way of Condescension & Lordliness unless

it was by a mediatour lest he should injure the marginal

Honour of his majesty & Justice

_____________________

entering into Cov. is a always either

the fruit of Peace between the parties cova-

nanting or agreeing or it is in order to Peace. but

there can be no Peace between G. & Rebels but

by a mediatour. G. & fallen men are

enemies G. as mans Lawgiver & Judge most ne-

cessarily appear as the Enemy of him who is under

the Guilt of sin & the sinners unless received

by a mediator will Remain an Enemy of G. The

II. Thing is to what cov. that is that is Spo-

ken of in the Text.

Answ. Tis that Cov. of Grace that X promul-

gated when he came unto the Θ. Tis the

cov. that G. has made with the ... Chh.

Vouched to the Xtian Chh as the Cov that G made

with his People at Mt. Sion. wherin Par-

don and Salvation are offered to Sinners &

promised to believers. The parties

covenanting are G. & believers. The Benefits

promised in this Covenant are

How the par that Eternal damnation that is

Sustained by the Law against sin & a

peace with G. a reconciliation after G. has been marginal

provoked & displeased & his Et. wrath deserted?

a deliv. from the Guilt of Sin

that G. should no more Look on those that

are interested in the Cov. as Sinners

having no Sin imputed to them and

that they should be delivered from Et. damnation

that is threatened by the Law to all Sinners

& from all other fruits of Gods wrath and curse

& that they shewd have a real interest in the

Righ. of X and acepted therin as perfectly

Righ. in the Eyes of G. the Judge of all & on

the account of that Righ. be received as the

objects of the favor of G. & unto the state

of the Children of G. ~~~ & that

their natures should be more & more Sanc-

tified that they should be delivered more

& more from sin & that they should

have the Image perfected in them

& that the word

enable them to live holy lives &

bring forth Good fruit & perservere in Holiness

to the End ~~~ & that he would in a way

of diligent & faithful serving him give them

the comforts of his Sp. peace of Conscience

& Joy in the Holy Gh. Special manifes- marginal

tation of his Glory & favour & a Joyfull

hope of Eternal Life. ~~~ & that he

would defend em from their Enemies that he

would be on their side as preserve em from

Satan & give em finally the victory over

all Enemies. ~~~ & that at death their souls

shall be perfectly freed from Sin shall ascend

to be with X in Paradise in a State of Glory

their bodies being still united to X resting in

their Savior till the Resurrection & that then

they shall Rise the souls being united to the

body again which shall be made like

to X’ Glorious body & that they in this

risen State shall be openly acquitted

before the Θ of angels & men hearing that

blessed sentence come -----------------

& that then they shall with soul and body

enter into the Kingd. that G. has prepared ----

------------- & shall Enjoy Et. Life in a

State of Perfect Glory & blessedness forever

& ever.

These are the Benefits promised in the

Covenant

& the

Jesus. whereby the believer does with all marginal

His heart close with X as the Savior

renouncing all other saviors & lovers &

giving up thems. to him to be united

to him & to be his in all his offices.

This Cov. is that which in SS is said to

a sure Cov. or a Cov. well

ordered in all things & sure & an Everlasting

Cov. The

III. Thing is to why this Cov. is called

a new Cov. To such an Enquiry

-----------

I consider - This is called a new Cov. with

Respect that old Cov. that was given forth

from Mt Sinai. Now is a Rela-

tive from and his relation to something

that in comparison of it is old when the SS. Speak

of a new Cov. there is plainly supposed to have been

some other Cov. in the ____ of which this suc-

ceeds . and still there is a Reference in this

to something that was of old at Mt Sinai

for in all the particulers mentioned in

the three verses of the text the comparison is

carried on between the two mountains of

Mt. Sinai & Mt Sion. at Mt Sion the new

cov. is given forth at Mt Sinai & the as the

old cov. was given forth from Mt. Sinai.

and that are two things that were marginal

Exhibited at Mt Sinai with Reference to which

the Cov. of Grace as Revealed by X under the

in the days of the Gosp. is called a new cov.

-----------------

1. The Cov. of works that G. had formerly

entered into with men in the state in which he

was first created was given forth at Mt.

Sinai. The Cov. of works was that

given forth to the Chil. of Israel. G. did not

properly then make a Cov. of w. with the

Chil. of Isr. because they were already fallen

he did not enter into a Cov. of W. with them

for they were not in & Capacity for it they were

already fallen Creatures & so not capable of Et.

Life by such a Cov. But yet G. did truly

give forthe the Cov. of w. from Mt Sinai

he did at that time renewedly reveal

and propose that Cov. of works to the

Chil. of Israel. From that mountain for

their Conviction to prove them & make them

sensible of their own miserable & undone Condi-

tion & make em sensible of

their necessity of a mediatour. So the Cov. of

works was given out to em in all the

strictness & severity to be as a Schoolmaster & marginal

Takes the Chh from its mi-

nority & fits it for the Coming of X. The Law

Shut em up to the faith that should afterward

be Rev. revealed

-------------

Tis Certain that the Cov. of works was then

given forth to the Chil. of Israel because

the SS Speaking of the ............

Cov. of Grace & comparing the Law & Gospel. &

the works of the Law with the faith of Jesus

X often Speaks of the Law as being given of

old by moses. now the Law

thus Spoken of is the very same thing as

the Legal Cov. & which is the same thing

the Cov. of w. The Law was given

at Mt Sinai with all its strictness &

terrors , with its thunders & lightnings & curses

saying cursed is every man that continues

not. Deut.27.26 Gal.3.10

____________

Now that is nothing else but giving forth

the Cov. of w. with its strict exaction of

Perfect Obed. on pain of Et. damnation.

2. The Cov. of Grace is delivered given forth by X marginal

is called a new Cov. with the

Respect to the same Cov. of Grace as Given forth

in its old Legal dispensation by Moses.

Tho the Cov. of works was given forth at

Mt. Sinai yet G. did not only that was not

the only Cov. that was given revealed there to

the Chil. of Israel. if it had it would have tended

to drive them into Despair. but the Cov.

But there was a Cov. that G. did not

only reveal but which he properly entered into

with that people at that mountain whereby

they became his Covenant People and

were as it were Espoused to hims..

and this could be no other than the Cov. of Grace.

G. did really reveal the Cov. of Grace at Mt

Sinai tho it was more ob-

scurely by far than X afterw. Revealed it &

tho it was delivered

in more Legal manner. G. did really renew

with the Chil. of Is. in the wild. That

Cov. that he had made with Abraham

430 years before. which the SS expressly

teaches to be the Cov. of Grace

& therf. G. when he comes to the people marginal

in the wil. encourages em with that that he

was the G. of Abraham. -

The Cov. of Grace is virtually contained in those

words in the Preface to the Ten C. Which words

G. Spoke at Mt Sinai I am the L. thy G. -

of. & the Cov. of Grace was

contained in the Ceremonial Law that was given

at Mt. Sinai wherin such sacrifices were

commanded & so many things representing &

shadowing forth the Redemption of X.

tho it was Essentially the same Cov. yet because

it differed . . . with that

The Cov. of Grace as promised by marginal

X in its Evangelical dispensa. seems

to be called a N.Cov. with Respect to those others

Constitutions on two accounts.

Because it succeeds them in order of time

& is Established in their stead. First the Cov.

of works is Revealed & then the Cov. of Grace &

first the Legal dispensation of the Cov. of Grace is

established & then the Evangelical. the Later

dispensation takes place of the former. the former

waxing old & vanishing away the Latter is Esta-

blished in its Stead. Heb. 8. 13. in that he

saith a new Cov. he hath made the first old now

that which decayeth & becometh old is ready to vanish

away.

2. It seems also to be called a new Cov. on account

of its Superiour Excellency because artificial things

are commonly in their best state while new &

because old things are removed to make way for new

therf. we find the word new sometimes denotes

the Great Excellency

of the thing that is said to be so. Thus X promises
