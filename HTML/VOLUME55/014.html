Heb. 12: 22. 23.24 ------- and to G. the

Judge of all. --------- 1740

we are come now to the

V. Particular in the Text. To shew how Xtians are

come to G. the Judge of all . .

---------

and in order to this I would

1. shew the person is here Spoken of.

2. How he in a peculiar manner it said to be

The Judge of all

3. How Xtians are said to be come to him as This

Judge of all. Copy on Trinity -----

I. It may be enquired who this is that is here

Spoken of. The words thems. shew that tis God

that is Spoken of but that don’t fully Resolve

the Question for there are more persons than

one that are G. G. the F. & Jesus & the

Holy Ghost are Each of them called G. in SS.

-------------

& sometimes when G. is Spoken of as one parti

cular person in the Godhead it intended more to

another. But what is intended is the deity or

the divine nature common to all that all the

persons in the Trinity & .. all the Persons

are Included. ~~~ But when G. is spoken

of in this place tis not the deity or the divine

Essence as common to all the Persons that seem

to be intended all the Persons of the Tri-

nity are not included. But the answer marginal

to the Enq. is that -

--------

G. the F. is the person Spoken of and is

here said to be the Judge of all tis not the

Son nor the Holy G. nor the deity as including all

the persons an but the Farther particularly &

this is manifest by the distinction that is used

in the Text between G. the Judge & Jesus the

mediator. ~~~ in the text Here .. Xions

are said to be come to G. the Judge of all & in

the next verse but one they are said to .. be

come to J. the mediator of the new covenant -

which makes it manifest that they are true dis-

inct persons that are Spoken of.

And the very

last distinction ... & the mutual Relation

of the two offices makes is manifeste that they

are two distinct persons that are Spoken one of

them a being the office or being the Part of Judge

and the other acting as mediator before

that Judge. Which shews the Person that

is called the Judge of all to be G.

the F. .....

for he is the Judge before whom X appears as marginal

Mediator. He is the Judge that of Lawgiver

& Judge that Required Satisfaction to be

made to the Law that appeal shew expects

a Righ. that shall answer the Law & he

is the Judge that to whom this satisfaction

is & Righteousness is presented & him

who accepts of it & he is the Person that ..

acquits & Justifies the Sinner in account

of X’ satisfaction & Righ. For tis the part

of a Judge to accept a satisfaction to a Law

and tis the part as a Judge ... to Justi

fy a person as having answered that Law and

being Righ. according to the Law. but this

way to matter may be more fully

explained under the next head viz that

II. Thing that was proposed which is to shew

how G. the F. in a particular manner is said to

be the Judge of all and the reason then

G. the ... as distinguished from the other Per-

sons of the Trinity.

In order the more fully to understand

this the following things may be noted.

---------------------

1. among the Three persons of the Trinity

Each one has his part & distinct office ... marginal

as it were his distinct office assigned him

by agreement among themselves. That

There is that which the SS Speaking of G. after the

manner of men represents as an agreement one

a Consultation and agreement among the Persons

of the Trinity. .... Respecting

the divine ... acts & disposals towards

men. Reasonable Creatures. ..... assigning

to Each one his part. in the management as

or distinct office in the management or ______

tions of divine Providence towards them ...

.....fallen men thus in Zech.6.13.

the Counsel of Peace is said to be between

the F. & the Son. ~~~ That disposition of

things among the Persons of the Trinity wheref.

Each one has his distinct office is what

divines commonly call the Economy

of the Persons of the Trinity.

2. By this agreement the Place or office of

G. the Father is to be the giver the defend-

& Judge of the Law. G. the F. by the

same agreement as Crea. of all the Reborn is

the person that is the maintain the Right.

of the Godless. that is to be his personal marginal

office .. maintain the authority &

majesty of the deity ~~~ & theref. it belongs to

him .. especially to be the Lawgiver

& to ... defend the divine Law to

see to it that the authority of the Law be

defended ~~~& .. do his peculiar office

not only to give the Law but as he ..

.. has the care of its defence he is the

Judge He is the Person that is to have

the Care of avenging Rebellion against the Law

& the dishonour done to it ~~~ & it belongs

to him to Judge when the Law is answered

as satisfied & to accept the Satisfaction.

------------------------

& therf when X as mediator under-

takes to ... make satisfaction to the Law

he made the Satisfaction to him the Father

the Father was the person to whom it belonged

to Require the Satisfaction. & to accept it be-

cause he was ...........

the Oerson whose office it was appointed to be the defender &

Judge of the Law ~~~ other wise it would

have been no more proper that the satisfaction

would be offered to G. the P. than to G. the

Son or the H.Gh.

deleted two lines marginal

As the office of the F. was to be the Person

defender & Judge of the Law so the office

that the Son was appointed to was to

be the mediator between G. & the Sinners

that had violated the Law. and the office

of the Holy Gh. was to be the ~~~~ He by appoint

ment was to be a person that in his office was

to act under the F. as being appointed by him

& interceding before him as paying a price to

him & seeking acceptance of him.

----------------

The office of the H.Gh. was to be the Emissary

or messenger of the other Persons. tis in his

office to act under both the others to Execute

what the others that which may is made by

the other two in that office to accomplish

the sucess of the negotiations that are between

the F. & the Son.

---------

These several distinct parts & offices, that here

are undertaken by each person of the Trinity as

mentioned according according to that covenant

which they have Entered into from Et. ~~~ & by

this Cov. .. tis peculiarly the

place of the F to be Judge of all. &

-----

it is as Judge that the mediator appears

before is was as Judge of the Law that marginal

G. the F. required satisfaction & it was

as Judge that he accepted the satisfaction

that X made. & it It was as Judge

that he Justified X and acquitted & Re-

warded him after he had made full satis-

cation for Sin & it is as Judge of the Law that G.

the Father aquitt acquits & Justified sinners

that believe in X. the Justification is

an act that peculiarly ~~~ Redemption belongs

to the Son but Justification peculiarly belongs

to the office of G. the F. X workt out a

Righ. for sinners, but tis G. the F. that

Justifies em for the Righ. & ~~~ but Justifica-

tion is properly the act of the Judge is an

act of a Judge .. Justifying and Condemning are

Judicial acts or the acts of a Judge :

3. It may be objected that G. is the Judge

of all neither of the other of the Persons

of the Trinity are. The other persons in

the Trinity in some sense may be said to Judge

men. The Holy Gho. doth so as he awakens

& convinces men of sin of Righ. & of Judgment

& brings em to Judge & Condemn them but

in this he only acts under the F. but setting marginal

home the Fathers Law upon them & con-

vincing them of his Righ. Judgements.

III. Place to Shew how Xtians are said to be

come to G. the Father as Judge of all &

in order to a Right. understanding of this it

must be . .

First noted in the Second that the apostle

here doubly has Respect to something

that is a distinguishing priviledge that

Xtians have by the Gospel in Cov. of Grace.

The Context makes this plain for all

these things from the beginning to his See are

distinguishing Priviledges that X have by the

Gospel ~~~ Xtians are not come the apostle

said to Mt Sinai But they are come

to Mt Sion the City of the living G. & c. -

via the Gospel Brings them to Privi-

ledges in that they could not be brought to by

the Law that was given at Mt Sinai. ~~ What

they are brought to at Mt Sion are the pri-

as distinguished from those th the Law that

Sion alone what the Chil. of

Israel were brought to at Mt Sinai . ...

as a Strict Lawgiver & Judge.

and those that never received or

To G. the Judge of all here to men-

tioned as the peculiar priviledge of Xtians

that they obtain by the Gosp. or Cov. of Grace marginal

& that they are brought to at Mt Sion

Here the Consideration of three things will

clere up their difficulty.

1. Tis to be considered. That G. ...........

In his proceeding with believers by the Gospel as

Cov. of Grace dont in the Last divest hims. of

the Character of a Strict Judge. Indeed there

are some things, that he does in the Character of

a Sovereign. But nothing to the Prejudice of

his other Character as a Strict Judge & as

Judge of the Law. ~~~ He appears under the

Gospel not only as sitting on a throne of Strict

Justice but also as sitting on a Throne of Sov.

Grace. But he ... don’t sit on a

Throne of Grace in such a manner or at all

to remove from the Throne of Justice he

acts as sitting on both those thrones in

the Fracious? promises of of the Gospel & in

the bestowance of the blessed priviledges of it

In Giving X to fulfill the Law for us G. asks

& in unity a new Cov. with men G. acts

as a Sovereign but these acts of Sovreignty

don’t at all prejudice his character as a marginal

strict Judge of the Law because he does some

thing herein that the Law did not direct

to yet he does nothing contrary to the Law

in it . ~~~ but after X is given and has full

filled the Law G. in bestowing the blessings

that X has purchased still acts as a Strict

Judge of the Law he in Justifying

& Rewarding believers in X does as much do

the part of a Strict JUDGE OF THE Law as

in Eternally Condemning Sinners

whom G. hath set forth.

G. in all that he has done for his people in

the way of the Cov. of Grace never has receded

from the Throne of his Justice and never

will. & does as much sit on the Throne

of Justice on Mt Sion as he did at Mt Sinai. Tho he don’t there sit

only on a Throne of Justice as he

did at Mt Sinai but also on a Throne

of Grace.

2. Tis the Great Priviledge that Xtians have marginal

by the Gosp. that tho G. still acts as

a Strict Judge yet they are brought to

acceptance & become a Strict union

with that Judge. Tho G. don’t at all

divest hims. of the Character of a Strict Judge

of the Law yet so through X those that have

... so is the matter wisely & wonderfully

ordered through X that those that have failed

and broken the Law are brought to this Great

G. so as to accepted of Him & received into

favor & the most Intimate union with

him & full Enjoyment of him acting as a

Strict Judge. ~~~ Thus tis the Pecu-

liar priviledge that Xtians have by

the Gosp. to come to G. the Judge

others that are under the Law can

not come night to this Strict Judge marginal

At Mt Sinai for as the apostle observes

in the 20. v. of the Context if so much as

3. Tho Xtians come to G. as a Judge

yet tis their peculiar priviledge not to come to

him as a Lawgiver. as man did under the

first law. Xtians or believers in X

appear before G. as a Judge as much as Ever

& G. acts as a Judge of the Law in Justi-

fying & Rewarding them for the Righ. of X

but they don’t come to G. as a Lawgiver

G. Gave man the Law to be fulfilled by

for him to fulfill and at mt Sinai

to Xtians at Mt Sinai is exacting a marginal

Receive the Reward of his obedience.

Xtians & Terrour & Conviction to others

Rejoicing to true Xtians.

What has been said shews the sure

Grounds that Xtians hopes of happiness
