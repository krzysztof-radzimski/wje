"Subjects of Inquiry" Subjects of Inquiry Jonathan Edwards

"Subjects of Inquiry"

A late manuscript booklet of memoranda outlining scholarly projects and study aims, "Subjects of Inquiry" provides clues to many intellectual interests Edwards was pursuing in the latter part of his life, from sketches for treatises to arguments about human nature and identity to thematic scripture reviews. Here also we see originally described the ideas for various notebooks such as "Defense of the Pentateuch as a Work of Moses" (an extension of "Notes on Scripture," no. 416), "Harmony of the Genius, Spirit, Doctrines, and Rules of the Old Testament and the New" (part of the larger project, "The Harmony of the Old and New Testaments," as described to the trustees of the College of New Jersey), and "Hebrew Idioms." Internal evidence suggests the book was constructed no earlier than September 1746, and entries date from the late 1740s through the end of his life.

*****

This notebook (BRBL, f. 12751) is twenty-two leaves, made mostly from discarded fan paper cut in irregular shapes, giving the book a butterfly effect when it lies open. For ease of presentation and reading, the text is here presented as entirely flush left. Leaf 13 is a discarded prayer bid. Leaves 2-12 were once numbered pp. 77-98 by Edwards as part of an index for "Types of the Messiah," but he abandoned this project (or completed it elsewhere), turned the pages over and re-numbered them for this booklet. Remains of the abandoned index are found on MS p. 24, with entries under the letters U-Z. The cover is made of brown ream wrapper and measures 3 3/4" x 7 3/4," inscribed front and back, "Subjects of Enquiry."

No copy of the first edition has been found, so the second edition, Glas' Works (5 vols., Perth, 1782-83) is used here. The Notes is in vol. 3, and the section to which JE refers is "Observes on the Sermon on the Mount," pp. 126-41. Glas argues (p. 127) that Jesus' references to "the law" in the Sermon are "quite abstracted from its particular application to the peculiar state of the nation of Israel, in the precepts and judgments of the covenant made with that nation at Sinai."
