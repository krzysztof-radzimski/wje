[170] He digged up and laid his founda. on a Rock.

Let all selfrighteousness be removed nothing be between you and

the rock. dig 'till you come to the rock. Let all the sand between

you and the rock be removed sand of your righteousness.

that you amy be settled fast on the rock.

_________

some sand remains if you make a righteousness of your

humility. of your Faith.

How persons ought to dig. ought to Labour and strive

hard in use of means. be thorough. descend low into

the Knowl. of your own Heart.
