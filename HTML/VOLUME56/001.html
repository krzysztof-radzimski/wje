3 If you follow these directions in all proba-

bility you will be converted [--] this

appears by Prov. 2. at beginning my son if ---

_____________________________________

These directions are agreable to Gods word.

& theref are his directions.

so that if you will follow them you will be in

Gos own way so that you that are desir-

ous of being Converted & are much concerned for fear

you should be damned you have [-] now heard of a

way how you may in all likelihood be converted

& may be delivered from hell & may be [---------]

better prepared for death & may be happy & blessed forever
