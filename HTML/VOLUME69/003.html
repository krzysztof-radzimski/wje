974. Matt. 13:47-50(i) 974.Matt. 13'47-50 (1_51)(1) trans Jonathan Edwards

St. Ind.Jan. 1750/51

__________________________________________

Matt 13. 47, 48, 49, 50 Again the Kingd

of Heaven is like unto a net.

_________

By the Kingdom of Heaven here is meant the Ch

whole Company of Christian Chh or the whole company

of Xtians all over the O.

__________

The People of X are the Kingdom of X They

because They are that Part of the O that be-

longs to X & that have X for their King.

___________

as King Georges People are his Kingdom They

being that Part of the O that He is King over

he rules over or that have Him for their King.

___________

Tis called the Kingd. of Heaven because X the

Kingdom is from Heaven & the Laws of his

Kingdom are all from H and the Countrey

the new Heart & new nature that his People

have is given Them is Holy & heavenly &

the Countrey they are to live in forever is

with X their King is Heaven

_____________________________________________________

Tis said that this Kingd of H is like a Net

that was Cast into the sea. The sea is the whole

O of mankind as a net that is cast into the

sea don't take all the Fish in the sea but

only goes round & fences in a few so the Kingd

of X dont take all the O but only a Part

______

As The Chh of X are separated from the Rest

of the O to be a peculiar People to Him to

be X's part

________

so as the net cast into the sea separates &

fences in & separates the fish that are in it

from all the Rest to bel that they may

belong to the fisherman & be his Part of

the Fish of the sea . while the rest are let

alone & are not meddled with.

___________________

The Fishermen that Cast the net are

ministers of the Gospel whom X appoints

to gather men into his Chh.

________________

X disciples several of Them were fishermen

__________________________________________________

by that was their trade that they got their living by. beforemarginal

___________________

by Trade and when X called them to follow

Him & to be his disciples & ministers

He told them that they should no longer

catch Fish but He would make em fishers

of men to Catch men

_____________

when a Fisherman casts a net into the

sea, what he aims at is only good Fish that

are good for Food He dont desire to catch

any but Good fish But yet the net will

gather every sort good & Bad.

____________

so none ought to come into the Xtian Chh.

but good men But because ministers Cant

Know mens Hearts bu every sort will come

in Good & Bad as there will be some Bad

Fish in the net so there will be some Bad

men in the Kingd. of X.

_________________________________________________

The Fishermen when their net was full

drew the net to sho fish out of the sea. to

__________________

the shore The Fish after they were in

the net were but a short Time in the sea

They were soon drawn out of the sea to the

shore so Those men that are in the

Xtian Chh & in the Kingd

so the Professours of Relig. will be but a

short time in this O They will soon

be taken away out of this O into the other

O.

_______

while the fish were in the net in the

sea it could not be seen what the Fish

were whether good or Bad but when they

were drawn to shore then it could be seen

what They plainly what they were &

the Bad could easily be distinguished from

the Good.

___________

so while Professours of the Xtian Relig. are

in this O oftentime we cant certainly say

marginal

what they be whether Good or Bad but

men are taken out of the O & when this O

comes to an End then it will be seen

_________________

'Tis said that when the Fish were drawn marginal

to shore men sat down & gathered the good into

vessels & cast the bad away

_____________________

When the fish were in the sea they Good

& bad were mixed together but it wont be

but when They were drawn to the shore they were

separated so in this O good men & bad are mixed

_____together but

G. will by no means suffer the wicked & the

Righ. alwaies to dwell together but will cer-

tainly separate em in another O

_________________

'Tis said that They the good were gathered

into vessels i.e. They were Preserved &

Kept as those that were prized & made much

of But the so G. will save his saints

& make much of Them as precious to Him

& the object as those that he dearly Loves

________________

But the bad Fish men cast away . as

those that were good for nothing.

_________

so in another O go God will cast away wicked

men He will have no Regard To em

they He will take no care of them but

will despise & hate em & as it were tram

ple em under foot if They have been

never so great & Rich men & have been

never so much honoured in this O yet G.

will be throw em away & trampl as it

were trample em under foot.

_________________

so shall it be says X at the End of the O

The angels shall come forth & sever the wicked

from among the Just

many are separated before the End of the O

They are separ when men die they go into marginal

another O where the Good are separated from

the bad but yet many are left in the O

where Good & bad are mixd together till

the End of the O

_________

But at the End of the O all shall be

separated. Then all bad the wicked shall

be separ separated from the among the Just

________

as soon when X appears coming in the

Clouds of Heaven ------ it will soon

be seen what men be ------- the difference

will presently be seen between Good &

Bad.

_________

X will then send his Angels to separate em

--- on his Right Hand --- Left.

_________

Tis said the wicked after they are thus severd

from among the just shall be cast into a

Furnace of Fire Hell is Tis

after the d. of Judgmt not only will the souls

of men be cast punished but their Bodies

which shall be raised from the dead shall be

thrown into a dreadful fire.

_______________

Tis called a Furnace of Fire because

to shew the fierceness of it Fire the Fur-

great Power & in the Fierceness of his ang great

who use of wrath for the wickedness of men

& therefore doubtless vastly more terrible than any fire

ever see in this O

___________________ ________________

Tis said there shall be wailing & gnashing

of Teeth to Represent the dreadful mi

_______________________

They will wail because their mis. will

be very dredful what & what they

cannot bear & also because they

never shall have any hope of being de-

livered but will Know that there will be

no End to their misery

Applicationmarginal

now I come to propose These things to your

serious Consideration

_____________

you have had the Gospel Preach'd to you.

The net has been let down and many of

you have been gathered in it & have brought

into in among the people of X into the Kingd.

of X.

But remember they that be gathered in the

net net are not all good the net [-] Ga-

thers of every Kind both Good & Bad.

& Good & & so tis to be feared they that

& so many of you that are called Xtians

are not all Good.

______________

Tis so tis feared that tis with you as tis

amongst the English & others that are called

Xtians . There are many more bad & than

Good.

_________

men cant certainly know how many of you are

good & how many are bad but G. Knows

_________

You may deceive men with a good outside

when your Hearts are rotten but you cant

deceive G.

_________

& Remember that als altho Good bad may

be are mixd together in this O - live together

in the same Houses They come to meeting

together & may sit down at the sacramt

together but they wont be mixed toge

ther alwaies.

________

The time will soon Come when they will

be G. will shew the Difference great

difference . by opening the Hearts of men &

that all the O may see what they be

when X comes at the End of the O theremarginal

the wick & the wicked shall be separated

from among the Just. Then many

of them that used to come to meeting together

& used to go eat together & drink together

& used to go hunting together shall .

be separated one from another never to

be together any more.

______________

X says In that day one shall be

two [-] shall be Guarding at a wall one

shall be ---- one bad .

many Parents shall then be separated from

Chil. --- Brothers & sisters --

_________

many Husbands & wives from their wives

& wives from their Husb. ---

_________

one shall be set on the Right H of X

& the other on the Left. ---- one with Glo.

angels -- other with devils . ----

_______

one shining forth as the sun --

The other appearing in all the Image of

the devil

___________

one To the one X will say.

come you blessed -- to the other

____

The one shall sing & R go into everlasting

Life ------- the other into everlasting fire

burnings into a furnace of fire that never

shall be quenched.

_______

Then shall that be fulfilled Isai. 65.

13. 14. Chap. Behold . my servants shall

rejoyce.

& rememb. what X says strait is marginal

the Gate & narrow is the way

There are may many will come in that

day & say L. L. have we not Eat

& drink drank in thy Presence

But He shall say -------------

__________

There are Therefore take Heed to your

selves that you do bent at last found

some of the bad fish that be cast

away.

see to it that your Hearts are right

with G.

dont Rest in outward shew but seek

a Get a Clean H. ----- a Holy Heart

that Hates all sin & Loves X &

& loves all the People of X & Loves all

X X

the ways of G.

__________________________________________________

unless you have a new Heart you never

----- will be Good

The & dont the think it enough only

to do some duties & to be good in some

things & live wickedly in other things

& but ha see that you have Respect to

all Gods Commands.

if That is the Reason that some

men Reform their lives for a little

while only & then never again.

------ Their Hearts were never Changed.

The Apostle Peter saysmarginal

They are like the dog that Re-

turns to his vomit.

______

The sow that is washed clean yet will be clean

but a little while because her nature is not

Changed & therefore she will go again & wallow

in the mire

____________________________________________________

But Remember that at the End of

the O when & the X will sever

the wicked from among the Just . &

cast em into a furnace of fire

He will search the Hearts of men
